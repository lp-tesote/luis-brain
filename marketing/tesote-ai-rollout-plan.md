# Tesote AI — Rollout Plan

## Goal

Mass rollout of Tesote AI focused on **Odoo customers** — biggest expected impact, finite addressable list.

## Strategy: two stages

**Stage 1 — Warm-list pilot (next 2–3 weeks).** Targeted send to qualified pipeline + existing customers on Odoo. Goal: learn what lands (objections, hooks, demo-to-conversion), generate early pipeline. Cheap learning before mass spend.

**Stage 2 — Mass rollout (after pilot signal).** Scale the refined assets to broader Odoo audience: paid + organic + Odoo partner channels in LATAM, dedicated `/ai` landing page, sales enablement, in-product activation for existing Tesote users on Odoo.

## Stage 1 — Warm-list pilot

### The video

- **Length**: 2 min exactly
- **Narrator**: Luis (personal, warm-send vibe — unfair advantage for a curated list)
- **Format**: Loom-style — face inset + screen-record demo (combines personal warmth with product proof)
- **Language**: Venezuelan Spanish, `tú`

#### 2-min structure

| Time | Beat | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Personal hook — "Hola, soy Luis. Te mando esto porque..." | Luis, full frame |
| 0:15–0:35 | The pain in one sentence (operar finanzas en Odoo) | Luis, full frame |
| 0:35–1:35 | **3 demo moments, ~20s each** — the most expensive minute, has to sing | Screen + Luis inset |
| 1:35–1:50 | Why now / proof (Mariel angle TBD) | Luis, full frame |
| 1:50–2:00 | CTA | Luis, full frame |

#### Demo moments (proposed, pending confirmation)

1. **Cross-entity question**: "¿cuánto facturamos este mes vs. el pasado?" → instant answer + chart (kills multi-Odoo-instance pain)
2. **AR / cobros query**: "muéstrame clientes con facturas vencidas > 30 días" → filtered table (kills the 10-click filter pain)
3. **Conversational follow-up**: "ahora dame el top 5 por monto" → context-aware (the part that feels like magic vs. a glorified search bar)

### Open decisions (Stage 1, video)

- [ ] **Demo moments** — confirm or revise the proposed 3
- [ ] **CTA** — reply-to-email (highest conversion, slowest) / calendar link (faster, more friction) / "Luis follows up personally" (you keep control)
- [ ] **Hook angle** — "small group, te elegí a propósito" (scarcity + flattery) vs. "llevas tiempo en Odoo, esto lo construimos pensando en ti" (relevance)
- [ ] **List assembly** — criteria? who pulls? (Vero / sales / Luis names?)
- [ ] **Distribution mechanism** — personal email from Luis / sales rep relay / WhatsApp (where most VE B2B happens)
- [ ] **Mariel case-study angle** — is she in for the proof line at 1:35–1:50?

## Stage 2 — Mass rollout (sketched, not yet scoped)

Candidate moves once the warm-list pilot returns signal:

- Dedicated `/ai` landing page on tesote.com — all paid + organic traffic lands here
- Social media — video cutdowns + carousels (LinkedIn-led for Luis's voice; channel mix for contadores TBD)
- Owned-list email beyond the pilot — broader Odoo customer + prospect base
- Live webinar / demo ("Talk to your Odoo data") — pipeline-grade, not just impressions
- Sales enablement kit for outbound + Odoo partner channels in LATAM
- In-product activation for existing Tesote users currently on Odoo
- Written case study with Mariel (if confirmed as design partner spotlight)
- PR / press in LATAM finance / tech outlets
