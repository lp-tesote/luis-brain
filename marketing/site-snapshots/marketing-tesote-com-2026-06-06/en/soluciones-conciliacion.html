Capabilities

Faster than your team, more precise than a human

Continuous reconciliation that learns your rules and proposes adjustments — no macros, no VLOOKUP.

Multi-criteria matching

Amount, date, reference, counterparty and hash. Configurable rules and continuous learning on your own history.

Prioritized exceptions

Tesote proposes adjustments with a confidence score, your team approves or rejects. No more digging through Excel.

Bidirectional sync

Every accounting adjustment reflects in the ERP in the same operation. No double entry, no lost files.

Complete audit trail

Every adjustment records user, applied rule, timestamp and reason. Ready for external audit, no reconstruction needed.

Live, not batched

Continuous reconciliation throughout the day. Differences surface in hours, not at month-end.

Auditable reports

Export every close with full traceability, fixed format and signed data — Big Four ready.
