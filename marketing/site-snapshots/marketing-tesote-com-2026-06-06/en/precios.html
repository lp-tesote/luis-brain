Plans · Tailored pricing

Pricing built for your operation, not for a generic table

Every finance team in Latin America runs differently. So instead of a public three-column pricing card, we design your plan on a call — based on your banks, volume, countries, and support tier.

Talk to an expert How it works
Why tailored pricing

Automated treasury is not a one-size-fits-all SaaS

A company with 3 banks in one country does not need the same plan as a regional group with 40 banks in five countries and two ERPs. Charging both the same would be unfair to either.

Pay for what you use

No per-seat fees, no shelf-ware. The plan reflects your real operation.

No invoice surprises

A fixed monthly or annual price agreed in the contract — no surprise charges per extra bank or report.

Grows with you

Your plan adjusts when you add a bank, a country, or an entity. No traumatic renegotiation.

Factors that shape your plan

What goes into the pricing conversation

These are the six factors that move price the most in an enterprise finance automation rollout across LATAM.

Number of banks

Each connected bank carries a maintenance cost — supervised connections, monitoring, and regional compliance. Tell us how many banks you operate and in which countries.

Transaction volume

Processing 5,000 transactions a month is not the same as 500,000. The plan is calibrated so unit cost drops as you scale.

Multi-country and multi-entity

Operating in one, two, or five countries changes regulatory, accounting, and reporting complexity. We work this out in discovery.

ERP integrations

SAP, NetSuite, Oracle, Profit Plus, Saint, and A2 Softway are ready out of the box. Custom or legacy integrations get scoped in the proposal.

Support tier

Standard support, premium with guaranteed SLAs, or a dedicated account manager — your choice based on operational criticality.

Dedicated implementation

The more complex the cutover, the more intensive the rollout. Companies with legacy migrations get a dedicated project team.

Always included

What is included, regardless of your plan

These capabilities ship with Tesote, always — they are the baseline of any agreement.

The full platform

Bank connectivity, smart reconciliation, 360° dashboard, reporting, and exports — no features locked behind an upgrade tier.

Enterprise security

End-to-end encryption, MFA, role-based controls, SSO, and audit trails. Regional compliance covered by default.

API and webhooks

A documented REST API, signed webhooks, and official SDKs. No paywall on access to your own data.

White-glove implementation

A dedicated project manager, in-house integration team, role-based training, and hyper-care at go-live.

Human support in Spanish

Support during LATAM hours, by humans who understand treasury and accounting — not a chatbot or a queued ticket.

Continuous updates

New banks, new connectors, and new features ship at no extra cost to every active customer.
