Case study

Grupo Mimesa: from a six-day close to a one-day close

How a Venezuelan retail chain wiped the "black Monday" off its finance calendar.

Let's talk See case studies

Grupo Mimesa runs 28 retail stores in Venezuela. Every monthly close required three analysts to spend six business days downloading transactions, reconciling in Excel, and arguing about discrepancies over email.

Results

What changed in 90 days

Three KPIs the CFO measures every month since go-live.

1-day close

From 6 business days to 1. The finance team got back a week each month for analysis and forecasting.

11 accounts, 5 banks, zero scrapers

Banesco, Mercantil, Provincial, BNC, and Banco del Tesoro consolidated through supervised connections.

92% auto-reconciled

Only 8% of transactions need manual review. Learned rules carry forward month after month.

The challenge

Before Tesote, the close was a quarterly operation disguised as monthly. Downloading bank statements took two full days. Reconciling against the local ERP consumed another three. And there was always an orphan transaction — a bank charge unrecognized by the ERP, a deposit in transit with no counterpart — that sparked an email chain between the accountant, the treasurer, and the store manager.

María González, CFO since 2022, sums it up: "We were closing the month on the 7th of the following month. For a board that wants reports on the 5th, that means we lived in extension-request mode. The constant feeling was that the business was moving faster than our ability to measure it."

The solution

In six weeks we connected the group's 11 active accounts, mapped the existing accounting rules, and trained the matching algorithms against three months of history. The first Tesote-assisted close took three days. The second, a day and a half. From the fourth onward, the close fits in a single workday.

No bank migration, no ERP swap. Tesote lives between the two systems, moves data both ways, and leaves the audit trail in order. Rules the team approves once apply automatically the following month.
