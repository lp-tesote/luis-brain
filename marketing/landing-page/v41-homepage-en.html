Frequently asked questions

Which banks does Tesote connect to?

120+ banks in Venezuela, plus banks in Panama, the Dominican Republic and the United States. You see all your accounts, in Bs and USD, from one place.

Does it integrate with my ERP or accounting system?

Yes. Tesote talks to your ERP in both directions: it pushes your already-enriched banking data and pulls your invoices to reconcile them and return posted entries — with no one copying and pasting.

How secure is my information?

Bank-grade encryption (AES-256-GCM, TLS, SHA-256 integrity), 2FA on every login, granular permissions and a complete audit trail of every action. We're in the process of SOC 2 Type II certification.

Does Tesote have access to my money?

No. Tesote doesn't hold your funds. Payments execute through your own bank's API and you authorize every action — Tesote sees and organizes, you decide and approve.

How long does implementation take?

We don't hand you a login and a manual: our team connects your banks, your ERP and your workflows with you. The time depends on the size of your operation, and we estimate it with you in the demo.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on your operation: number of banks and entities, volume and the scope of automation you need. We define it together in the demo, based on what your team requires.
