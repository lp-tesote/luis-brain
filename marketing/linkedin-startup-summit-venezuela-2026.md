# LinkedIn Post — Startup Summit Venezuela (Miami 2026)

Honored to have participated in the Startup Summit Venezuela in Miami this year. Last year's was great, but this time felt different.

For most founders, investors, and operators who've been operating in Venezuela the past few years, we've been dealing with the cards we were dealt — not expecting free lunches. It's not like we were waiting for a tectonic change to just happen and boom, all of a sudden everything is great and we're in business. Absolutely not.

What this event made clear is that the group of people who've had serious boots on the ground have been building great companies from the ground up, with a first-principles mentality. And now, with some winds at our back, we're perfectly prepared and positioned to take these opportunities to the next level.

One phrase that's been resonating a ton is the change of mentality happening in real-time — going from a resilient mindset to an aggressive, growth-oriented, competitive one. And we're here for it.

I couldn't be more excited about the opportunity in front of us. Some of us never thought we'd live to see this, but here we are, and it's ours for the taking. Let's go.
