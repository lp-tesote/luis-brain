# Team Announcement — Tesote AI

---

Team —

Wanted to formally announce **Tesote AI**, what it does, and how we're rolling it out.

Quick context: Tesote AI lives inside our product (the "finance chief on call"), and also runs on top of a customer's existing Odoo via our MCP — no migration required. We're bullish this is going to be transformational, and the first customer reactions back that up.

- Fivenca
- Segven

We haven't told them yet, but we will in the next few days.

**3. Pipeline acceleration.** Two cohorts to hit ASAP:

We should do a session with the sales team to identify reactivation candidates — especially the ones already on Odoo.

We also want to move quickly so we can launch publicly soon and **own the narrative**: Tesote AI is the first AI for Venezuelan businesses.

Excited to have everyone behind this. Let's go!
