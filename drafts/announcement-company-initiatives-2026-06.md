# Company-Wide Announcement — Current Initiatives

Company-wide version of the [[slack-dan-current-initiatives]] DM (sent to Dan 2026-06-03).
Refreshed against the week of Roam calls (2026-06-08 → 06-10) and reframed around the
**four pillars** from the finance planning call: implementation, bank migration, finance, product launch.

Source: [[../strategy/current-initiatives-2026-06]].

Team — putting in writing where we're driving over the next month so we're all pulling in the same direction. A lot has landed in the last few weeks, and it's converging into **four priorities.** These aren't separate tracks — they stack: implementation and the bank migration make the product reliable, finance buys us the room to move, and the launch is how we turn all of it into growth.

*1 — Implementation: make onboarding our edge*
• We're upgrading the implementation process and treating it as an *extension of sales*, not an afterthought — including incentives for clean, fast implementations.
• Putting the sales dossier into practice to tighten the sales → implementation handoff.
• Fixing our instrumentation (PostHog, user *and* workspace-level tracking) so we stop losing users we can't see today.

*2 — Bank migration: scrapers → bank APIs*
• Batch 1 is kicked off. We picked the first clients by MRR × # of entities × heaviest usage of BBVA, Mercantil, Banesco, and Bancaribe.
• This is the load-bearing bet — more reliable connections and far less scraper firefighting. Client communications about the change are going out.

*3 — Finance: build the runway*
• Getting to a clear break-even picture and the cash reserve / financing to get there *without slowing down*.
• Automating internal finance ops and adding capacity where we're bottlenecked.

*4 — Product launch: Tesote AI (~Jul 1)*
• Relaunching with Tesote AI as a standalone product, plus a redesigned web and dashboard.
• Marketing campaign + internal AI video. QA is the gate before we ship.
• In parallel: continuing to test the payments suite and building out the portal.

Each lead will go deeper on their area. The short version: we know where we're going and how the pieces fit. Let's go.
