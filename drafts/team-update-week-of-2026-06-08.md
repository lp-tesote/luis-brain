# Week of June 8 — Top Company Priorities

Big week. Here's where the company is focused and what moved.

## The three priorities

**1. Tesote AI + new web launch — July 1.**
Confirmed at Wednesday's all-hands: AI and the web redesign launch together on July 1. Initial AI scope is Tesote data queries; pricing will be usage-based with limits. QA is the gating workstream, and AI infrastructure is separated from core so nothing destabilizes the product clients rely on. Marketing goes aggressive alongside: teaser + free-trial campaign, simplified contracts and subscription terms. Expect office presence to matter a lot in the weeks around launch.

**2. Banking API migration.**
Formal project kickoff happened Tuesday. Priority banks: Provincial BBVA, Mercantil, Banesco, Bancaribe. Priority clients for batch 1: Kasea, Kangur, Grupo Rica, JUMPY, Grupo Abreu, Yomi, Leiros. T-1 data confirmed acceptable for most clients. Mercantil + Provincial are critical for both sales and implementation. Estefany is automating bank form fill-in; a dedicated comms channel for the migration is being set up.

**3. Client communication.**
Bank-connection changes affect real clients and we will get ahead of it: mass communication on connection changes is being prepared, and Estefany is drafting the July 1 user-facing comms with a targeted list of power users and project leads. The framing is a value-prop transition, not just a feature announcement.
