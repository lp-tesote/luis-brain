Auto-pilot

Graduate trusted patterns from Assist (you approve every one) to Auto-with-review (Tesote posts, you skim a daily digest) to Autonomous (handled end-to-end, you only see exceptions).

Assist (default)

AI suggests, you approve every one. Used for new patterns and anything that hasn't earned trust yet.

Auto-with-review

Tesote posts automatically. You skim a daily digest and can revoke anything that looks off.

Autonomous

Fully automated. You only see exceptions and a weekly summary. Used for high-volume, low-variance flows.

Trust guardrails

Global rules that hold even for autonomous patterns. If any of these fire, the transaction comes back to your queue regardless of mode.

Transaction amount > $10,000 USD always requires manual approval
New counterparty (first seen in last 30 days) always requires manual approval
Period close (after day 20 of month) pauses all autonomous posting
Variance from rolling average > 40% for this pattern
Demo notes
  • This is where the "Assist → Auto-with-review → Autonomous" PRD philosophy becomes concrete UI. Controllers earn their leverage here, one pattern at a time.
  • "Promote to Autonomous" is the controller's choice — Tesote never moves patterns up modes without explicit approval.
  • High-$$ patterns (payroll) get a yellow flag and only let you graduate to Auto-with-review, not Autonomous.
  • "Trust guardrails" are the global escape hatches — even Autonomous patterns get bounced back to the queue if any guardrail fires. This is the defense against the "AI errors are catastrophic in accounting" risk in the PRD.
