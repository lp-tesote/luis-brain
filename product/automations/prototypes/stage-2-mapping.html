Demo notes (delete before client)
  • The dedup screen is the messiest moment in onboarding — most VE clients have 30–40% duplicate counterparties in Odoo. This is the wow moment ("we cleaned 38% of your vendor list").
  • RIF (legal tax ID) match is the strongest dedup signal. The UI calls it out explicitly.

  • The bottom mapping table is the *result* the controller takes away — clean counterparties they'll see in daily work, with Odoo partners hidden but preserved for the mapping layer.
