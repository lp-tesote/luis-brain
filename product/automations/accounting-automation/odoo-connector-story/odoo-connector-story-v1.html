how the tesote ↔ odoo connector works

Two systems,
one truth.

Banking data lives in Tesote. The books live in Odoo. The connector is the translation between them — reading one way, posting the other, every single day.

01 · Where it starts

The bank truth is already here.

Before anything touches Odoo, Tesote already holds the cleanest possible record of what actually happened in the bank. Connect pulls every account, at every bank, daily — normalized, deduplicated, multi-currency. Most integrations start at the ERP and fight for bank data. We start with the bank data and walk up into the ERP.

02 · The pull

We read the whole accounting graph.

The connector isn't a blind pipe. Tesote reads the client's full Odoo accounting state — chart of accounts, partners, invoices, journal entries, statements, taxes, analytic accounts. Without read access we'd be guessing; with it, Tesote can render exactly what the books look like — what's coded, what's open, what's reconciled.

03 · The key distinction

The business and the accountant speak different languages.

This is the most important design decision in the whole connector. Tesote categories and Odoo's chart of accounts are not the same thing, and forcing them to be ruins both.

Tesote · categories
Operaciones > Logística > Combustible
How the business thinks
Hierarchical, business-language, built for a non-accountant to navigate daily. This is the taxonomy the team — and the AI — works in.
Odoo · chart of accounts
6.2.1 — Combustibles y Lubricantes
How the books think
Flat, numeric, GAAP-aligned, often opaque to everyone but the accountant. It stays in Odoo, exactly where it belongs.

Tesote is the source of truth for how the finance team thinks. Odoo is the source of truth for what the books say. The export between them is a translation — not a sync.

04 · Categorize independently

Tesote never waits for Odoo.

Because the taxonomy is Tesote-native, the team codes transactions the moment they land — no round-trip to the ERP, no "create the vendor in Odoo first," no dependency on the chart of accounts being clean.

A transaction lands from the bank.
already normalized by Connect — date, amount, reference, raw description
Rules + AI suggest a category and counterparty — in Tesote's language.
the AI suggests "Operaciones > Logística > Combustible", never "account 6.2.1" — explainable to a human, decoupled from any client's CoA
The team approves, edits, or rejects. New counterparties get created on the fly.
every correction is training data; high-confidence flows graduate to auto-pilot

05 · The mapping layer

The translation is the integration.

Between the two languages sits a per-client mapping, configured at onboarding, editable forever, and versioned. When a coded transaction posts to Odoo, the mapping translates every Tesote primitive into its Odoo counterpart — deterministically.

06 · The push

Yesterday, posted automatically.

The write-back runs on a T-1 cycle: once a banking day closes, Tesote takes the day's settled movements, runs them through the mapping, and posts them into Odoo as bank statement lines — no one uploads a file, no one re-keys a movement.

Day T · banking hours
Movements settle
Connect captures them live in Tesote; the team codes in Tesote's taxonomy as they land.
Overnight
Translate + post
The mapping converts categories, counterparties, taxes; the connector writes each movement to the right Odoo journal.
Day T+1 · morning
Books are current
Every statement line for yesterday sits in Odoo — clean, attributed, auditable.
T−1
The books are never more than one day behind the bank. Already live in production — this was Phase 1, the part that proved we can write to Odoo reliably. Everything else in this story is what gets built on top of a pipe that already works.

08 · Why this shape

Three ways to connect two systems. Two of them fail.

Every bank-to-ERP integration picks one of three architectures, whether it knows it or not:

Thin UI
Everything lives in Odoo; Tesote is a face.
Every action waits on Odoo being set up correctly first — the vendor must exist, the account must exist. The friction kills the daily workflow.
no leverage
Parallel sync
Two systems, two truths, sync in between.
Every conflict is a support ticket. Audit trails diverge. The books and the workflow drift apart until nobody trusts either.
constant drift
Mapping layer · ours
Tesote owns the workflow. Odoo owns the books.
The team works in business language; Odoo receives clean translated entries. It's the Ramp playbook — categorize in the product's taxonomy, map to the GL once, never show a clerk an account number.
the ramp model
09 · Where this goes

A pipe today. A cockpit next.

Push is live. Pull is in progress. The third phase is write-back from the cockpit: coding queues, reconciliation, AP automation — every finance action originating in Tesote and landing in Odoo as the right object. The connector is the foundation that earns us the right to build it.

Reconciliation is where the starting asset pays off twice: Tesote matches its own clean bank lines against the journal entries it pulled from Odoo — amount, date, counterparty, reference — and posts confirmations back. We reconcile with better bank data than Odoo could ever get on its own.
