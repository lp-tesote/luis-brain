Recommended demo path

  1. Stage 0 — show CS what a kickoff brief looks like before they ever pick up the phone
  2. Stage 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 — walk the controller's onboarding experience in 30 days
  3. Stage 5 (Queue) — toggle role between Controller and Clerk; flip the CoA column; expand the Netflix row
  4. Cap #3 (Recon) — expand the Distribuidora Caracas row; show match signals
  5. Stage 6 (AP) — show three-way match callout on Distribuidora Caracas invoice
  6. Stage 7 (Dashboard) — north-star KPI and close checklist with post-v1 capabilities clearly marked
  7. Stage 8 (Auto-pilot) — the graduation flywheel + trust guardrails

HTML + Tailwind + Inter. Self-contained — no build step. Lightweight JS only for the role toggle and CoA column on the queue. Realistic VE data throughout (RIFs, USD/Bs amounts, Spanish vendor names).

Each prototype has a "Demo notes" footer with reviewer guidance — delete before showing to clients.
