# Customer Research

ICP exploration, segment thinking, and target lists for Tesote Pay. Where we figure out **who** we sell each rail to (and why), separate from `rails/` (the bank-side plumbing) and `plans/` (the product specs).

The market splits three ways by who the Tesote customer collects from:

- **B2B** — Tesote customer collects from other companies. Needs the juridico → juridico rail, currently bank-gated.
- **Retail** — Tesote customer's collections are mixed/retail-flavored. Mostly juridico → juridico today.
- **B2C** — Tesote customer collects from individuals. Juridico → natural rail is **live and tested**. Sellable today.
