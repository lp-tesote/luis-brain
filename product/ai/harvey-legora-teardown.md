# Harvey + Legora — product teardown

> Companion to [[winning-vs-horizontal-ai]]. The strategic frame there is derived from category-level patterns; this doc is where we ground those patterns in *what these products actually do today*.

## Why this doc exists

[[winning-vs-horizontal-ai]] claims seven patterns Tesote should steal from Harvey + Legora. Those claims are derived from public material I had at hand — not from a focused product look. Before any of this becomes investor narrative or team direction, the patterns need verification.

## What to look at

### Harvey

- Product page: harvey.ai
- Assistant / workflow products — what's the IA? How are workflows discovered?
- Citation UX — how does a Harvey answer surface its sources?
- Pricing / packaging — what do they sell? per-seat? per-firm? per-workflow?
- Demo videos / customer case studies (Allen & Overy, PwC published walkthroughs)
- Recent product launches (LinkedIn / their blog) — what shipped in 2025/2026?

### Legora

- Product page: legora.com
- The review/spreadsheet UI — screenshots, demo flow
- Drafting / collaboration features
- Multi-user / firm view
- Geographic strategy (Nordic origin, EU expansion, US push?)
- Pricing / packaging

### Comparative

- Where do they overlap, where do they diverge?
- Which one has the cleaner "land-and-expand" motion?
- Who's winning what segment (big-law vs mid-law vs in-house vs verticals)?

## Patterns to verify

From [[winning-vs-horizontal-ai]]:

1. **Workflows, not chat** — confirm both lead with workflow products, not chat
2. **"Do this to N things" primitive** — verify Legora's table UI is real and central
3. **Source-grounded outputs** — every output cites
4. **Speak like the user** — domain vocabulary, document types
5. **Multi-player by default** — firm-level, not personal copilot
6. **Land-and-expand via reference accounts** — verify the GTM motion
7. **Narrow + deep** — confirm small workflow count, deep depth

For each, write: *what they actually do* + *how literally we should copy it*.

## What we explicitly should NOT copy

- Brand voice — legal is more formal than finance-ops; controller-in-VE has its own register
