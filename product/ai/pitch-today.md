# How to pitch Tesote AI — today

> **The job of this doc.** Make Luis pitch-ready for next week. Not the strategic positioning ([[positioning-the-finance-chief]]) — that's the *why*. Not the deep capability tour ([[pitch-agents-plus-ai]]) — that's the *catalog*. **This is the playbook for the next 30-60 days of prospect conversations**: what to say, in what order, what to claim, what to roadmap, what NOT to overclaim. Will rev every 2 weeks as we ship.

---

## The two beats that have to land in every conversation

Today's pitch is two beats. Both have to land. One without the other fails.

### Beat 1 — Posture: "Your finance chief is on call."

> *Tu nuevo chief financiero está de guardia. Sabe SENIAT, sabe IVA, sabe retenciones, sabe FX. No hace falta entrenarlo. Empieza mañana.*

The chief that every LATAM company can't afford to hire — opinionated, jurisdiction-fluent, never sleeps. Today they summon it; soon it walks in unannounced (that's the 60-day roadmap). The capability is real today; only the trigger is reactive.

Why this beat: *who* it is. The persona makes the leverage trustworthy.

### Beat 2 — Scope: "The possibilities are almost infinite."

> *Lo que antes tomaba un millón de clicks, ahora son 30 segundos y un prompt. Reportes, conciliaciones masivas, categorizaciones, facturas, descuentos, cierres, cobranzas, libros de IVA, comprobantes — todo lo que históricamente fue molesto en finanzas, ahora es fácil. Es el antes y después en la historia de finops en Venezuela.*

The chief doesn't just *advise* — it *executes at scale*. Mass reconciliations. Mass categorizations. Mass invoice generation. Mass retention application. Bulk anything. One prompt against thousands of rows. Things that used to be a controller-week now take a coffee.

Why this beat: *what* it does. The leverage makes the chief useful daily, not just impressive on a demo.

### Why both beats together

| Just the posture | Just the scope | Both together |
|---|---|---|
| "Smart chatbot that knows SENIAT" | "AI that bulk-edits invoices" | "**The chief that knows SENIAT and edits 80 invoices at a time**" |
| Impresses for 5 minutes | Impresses for 10 minutes | **Changes how the buyer thinks about their finance team forever** |
| Renews 40% | Renews 60% | **Renews 90%, refers 3x** |

## The historic claim — earn it, then say it

The line you actually deliver at the end of the demo:

> *"Los equipos de finanzas en Venezuela no van a operar igual después de Tesote AI. Este es el antes y después de finops en Venezuela."*

This is a category-defining claim. **Don't open with it** — earn it across the 15-minute demo, then drop it as the closing line. If you open with it, you're a salesman. If you close with it after they've seen the chief grade their own books and run a mass reconciliation, you're stating a fact.

## The "almost infinite" scope — concrete enough to be credible

"Infinite possibilities" is hollow without a menu. Here's the menu to keep loaded in your head. Each item is a thing the AI does today (✅) or in the next 60 days (🟡) — refer back to [[pitch-agents-plus-ai#what-we-still-need-to-build-vs-show-today]] for the function-by-function readiness table.

### Local intelligence — "esto es lo único que ChatGPT no puede hacer por ti"

## Today / 30 days / 60 days — what to claim, what to roadmap

The deck's last slide. Customers buy the calendar, not the vision.

### ✅ Today — installs this week, works in the first session

- "Your chief is on call. Open the chat, ask anything."
- All ✅ items in the scope menu above
- The 6 flagship workflows that map to live capability
- LATAM-fluent: SENIAT-aware, multi-entity, FX-aware, retention-aware
- Single-tenant on your Odoo + your bank rails
- Power-user mode: same MCP in Claude Desktop / Cursor for the technical buyer

### 🟡 Next 30–60 days — dated, not vague

- **Morning brief / daily report card** — the chief stops waiting to be asked. Grades + the day's prosecutions arrive at 7am.
- **Pre-auditoría SENIAT full 30+ checks** — today it's the 5-check version
- **Bulk retention back-application + bulk comprobante generation** — partial today, full in 60 days
- **AP autopilot (Bandeja Mágica + pay-run agent)** — inbound inbox v1 in build
- **Productized close package** — *"ciérrame mayo"* runs end-to-end

Name a target month in the deck. *"Next sprint"* / *"end of Q3"* / *"by [date]"* reads as discipline. *"Soon"* reads as vaporware.

### 🔵 Roadmap — H2 2026

- **Autopilot mode** — the chief acts on its own grades. Drafts the cobranza, queues the retention, schedules the brief — all without prompt. You approve, it executes.
- **Anomaly alerts before you ask** — exception-based prosecutions, push-style.
- **Multi-jurisdiction grading rubric** — DGII (DR), DIAN (CO), SAT (MX), IRS (US).
- **CEO weekly brief** — auto-delivered to the inbox of whoever should see it.

The roadmap slide is where the word *"vision"* honestly applies. Not before.

## The 15-minute demo — exact script

### Minute 0–1: positioning slide

Open with the line. No preamble.

> *"Nadie en Venezuela puede pagarse un CFO del calibre que tu empresa necesita. Nosotros vamos a ser ese CFO para ti. Está de guardia 24/7. Habla SENIAT. Empieza mañana."*

Then: *"Te voy a mostrar 3 cosas en los próximos 12 minutos. Si después de eso no estás convencida, no te robaré más tiempo."*

### Minute 1–4: first prosecution (the LATAM moat moment)

Type into the live AI, on their pre-loaded data:

> *"Encuentra todas las facturas que no tienen factura fiscal anexa. Si las registramos como gasto deducible y nos audita SENIAT, ¿qué riesgo tenemos?"*

What lands: the AI names the invoices, the amounts, the deducción at risk, and the proposed action. The buyer realizes the AI *understands SENIAT* — not just accounting.

**The line you deliver after:**
> *"Esto no lo hace ChatGPT. Lo hace Tesote AI porque está entrenada con la normativa local."*

### Minute 4–8: second prosecution (the scope moment)

> *"Concilia todos los movimientos del estado de cuenta de Banesco de este mes contra las facturas abiertas."*

What lands: 30 seconds, 80+ matches, 6 exceptions surfaced with reasons. The buyer mentally calculates the hours.

**The line:**
> *"Lo que tu equipo hace en 4 horas, Tesote AI lo hace en 30 segundos. Las posibilidades son casi infinitas — reportes, categorizaciones, cobros, retenciones, lo que sea — un prompt, segundos."*

### Minute 8–12: third prosecution (the diagnostic moment)

> *"¿Por qué llevamos 4 meses sin cobrarle a [biggest customer]? Mándale cobranza ahora mismo, en mi tono."*

What lands: AI pulls the aging, drafts the WhatsApp in the buyer's voice, queues for approval. The buyer feels the loop close: ask → execute → done.

**The line:**
> *"Esto no es un asistente. Es un chief que ejecuta. Tú apruebas; él trabaja."*

### Minute 12–14: the calendar slide

> *"Hoy se instala. Hoy preguntas y ejecuta. En 30 días llega solo en las mañanas con tu reporte y sus alertas. En 60 días corre el cierre mensual completo. En 2026 H2 actúa sin que le preguntes, con tu aprobación final."*

### Minute 14–15: the historic close + hand over keyboard

> *"Los equipos de finanzas en Venezuela no van a operar igual después de esto. Este es el antes y después de finops en Venezuela. Escribe lo que quieras. Cualquier cosa que se te ocurra."*

The wedge: if they type a second prompt unprompted, they're sold.

## Picking the 3 prosecutions per prospect

The demo flexes on the prospect. Pre-run a 1-day data pull on their Odoo (with permission, single-tenant) and pick prosecutions that hit *their* sore spots:

| Prospect profile | First prosecution | Second prosecution | Third prosecution |
|---|---|---|---|
| **High AR / collections pain** (Casagri-style) | Aging diagnostic | Mass cobranza | Subscription gap analysis |
| **High volume / categorization pain** (distributors) | Mass categorize 3 months | Rule generation from patterns | Anomaly detection |
| **SENIAT / compliance pain** (large industrials) | Factura fiscal audit | Retention gap detection | Pre-auditoría IVA |
| **FX pain** (exporters / dollar-cost-base) | FX loss per invoice | Pricing margin recompute | BCV-vs-diferencial analysis |
| **Multi-entity** (groups, holdings) | Counterparty 360 across entities | Cross-entity duplicate cleanup | Consolidated close package |

The prosecutions hit harder when they're on the prospect's data, not on demo data.

## What NOT to claim today

The pitch dies if any of these are overclaimed and the buyer catches it:

- ❌ "It runs on autopilot." — Today it's on-call. The autopilot is 2026 H2.
- ❌ "It moves money on its own." — It drafts; you approve. Anything money-touching is supervised. Forever. (This is also the [[pitch-agents-plus-ai]] posture.)
- ❌ "It works on any ERP." — Today: Odoo + Tesote Workspace. SAP/SAGE/MS Dynamics is roadmap.
- ❌ "All 30+ SENIAT checks run today." — Today it's 5. Full set is 🟡 in 60 days.
- ❌ "Multi-country today." — VE-native today. DR is the next jurisdiction.
- ❌ "Replaces your controller." — Raises the bar on your controller. The controller is the daily user, not the enemy.

If you wouldn't bet your reputation on the claim, soften it. The historic claim ("antes y después de finops en Venezuela") is bet-able. Don't dilute it with claims that aren't.

## Common objections — answers in advance

**"How is this different from ChatGPT?"**
> *"ChatGPT no sabe que tu factura necesita una factura fiscal anexa para ser deducible. No sabe quién es contribuyente especial. No tiene acceso a tu Odoo, ni a tus movimientos bancarios, ni a tus retenciones. Esto sí — y por eso ejecuta, no solo conversa."*

**"What if it makes mistakes?"**
> *"Toda decisión que toque plata necesita tu aprobación. El chief no mueve un bolívar sin que tú aprietes el botón. Pero también: cada respuesta muestra de dónde sacó los datos — la trazabilidad es parte del producto."*

**"My team won't trust an AI with our books."**
> *"Tu equipo no le va a entregar las llaves. Le va a delegar las tareas que ya odian — la conciliación, la categorización, los recordatorios de cobro. El chief les quita el 80% aburrido. El 20% que requiere juicio, lo siguen haciendo ellos — pero ahora apoyados."*

**"How long is implementation?"**
> *"Hoy. La conversación que estamos teniendo ahora puede terminar con el chief corriendo en tu Odoo esta misma semana. Si necesitamos un 'proyecto de implementación' es que el producto está mal."*

**"What about data privacy?"**
> *"Single-tenant. Tu data no se comparte, no se entrena, no sale. Tu Odoo, tus rails bancarios, tu workspace — aislados. Es el mismo modelo de seguridad que ya tienes con Tesote."*

**"What does it cost?"** *(Reference: [[pricing]])*
> *"$1.5k/mes el Workspace, $1.5k/mes el Odoo, $2.5k/mes el bundle. Sin overage durante los primeros 6-12 meses mientras calibramos. Si lo comparas con el sueldo del CFO que no puedes contratar — el ROI es obvio."*

## Sequencing — meetings 1, 2, 3

| Meeting | Goal | What you show | Buyer in the room |
|---|---|---|---|
| **#1 — 15-min demo (this doc's script)** | Get the *"can I have this today?"* line | Three prosecutions on their data + the calendar slide | Controller + CEO |
| **#2 — CFO depth (45 min)** | Earn procurement | Multi-entity close demo + counterparty 360 + brief for CEO + pricing | CFO + Controller |
| **#3 — Compliance & legal (30 min)** | Close the loop | Pre-auditoría SENIAT walkthrough + audit trail + data isolation + DPA | Finance director / legal |

If meeting #1 doesn't get one of the magic lines — *"can I have this today?"* / *"this isn't a roadmap?"* / *"what's it cost?"* — pause and recalibrate before meeting #2. Don't push a buyer who isn't pulled. (Same test as [[pitch-agents-plus-ai]].)
