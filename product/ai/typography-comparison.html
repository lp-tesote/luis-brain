Tesote AI — Typography

Three takes on the same chat pane. The content, palette, layout, and component shapes are identical. Only the type system changes. Scroll between them. Squint. The one that recedes is the one to ship.

companion to typography-spec.md · snapshot 2026-05-20

How to read this. The current build (variant 1) uses Inter Tight at 14px with weights pushed to 700 on the pane title. That's the headline cut of Inter, designed for badges and chrome — not for body. When you stack heavy weights on a tight cut, every surface starts leaning forward at you.

Variants 2 and 3 cap weight at 500, bump body to 15px, give the chat 1.6 line-height, and introduce one moment of Instrument Serif in the empty-state hero — the editorial gesture that signals craft without shouting. Variant 2 keeps the sans on Inter (zero migration cost). Variant 3 swaps to Geist (more modernist, more "AI-native" company).

The squint test

Scroll to the top. Squint at variant 1, then variant 2, then variant 3. Look for these signals:

  • Variant 1 stays sharp. Inter Tight at body size plus 600/700 headers keep every surface "active." The chat fights for your eye.
  • Variant 2 blurs into something quiet. Same content, but the chat recedes. The serif moment in the hero is the only place that asserts. This is the move.
  • Variant 3 has more personality. Geist's geometric character makes it feel more "made by an AI company." Slightly less safe than Inter, slightly more differentiated from Mercury/Stripe/Brex.

The biggest single lever is dropping Tight and capping at weight 500. That alone is ~80% of the perceived shift. Bumping body to 15px and adding the serif moment carry the rest. Total CSS delta to ship: ~50 lines.
