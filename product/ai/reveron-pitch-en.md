In the more than two years since we launched Tesote, we've received a huge number of requests from our clients: custom reports, bulk functionality, specific automations, unique formats, complex analyses, and many other needs around the banking information they handle every single day.

Over that time we've built many of those features — but we also came to understand something important: if we kept solving each request as an isolated feature, we would never move at the speed our clients need.

So over the past few months we decided to build something with a far greater capacity for impact. Not one more feature. Not one more report. But a new layer of intelligence on top of all the information that Tesote already centralizes automatically.

Today we're introducing **Reverón**.

Reverón is Tesote's enterprise AI, built in our **Tesote AI** lab, and designed specifically to help Venezuelan companies understand, analyze, and act on their financial information.

Reverón's first mission is to bring light to the banking information that Tesote already organizes for you. To take information that is flat, static, and hard to exploit, and move it from 2D to 3D: alive, queryable, actionable, and automatable.

Reverón works in three main ways.

First, through **recurring reports and automations**. This covers much of the day-to-day work: the predictable, the repetitive — the things that don't require deep analysis but do require consistency, punctuality, and precision. For example, a weekly report sent automatically every Friday with balances, movements, relevant variances, the most active accounts, and the important alerts of the week.

Second, through **complex requests**. Here we can ask Reverón for analyses that would normally take a long time in Excel or require technical support. For example: "Calculate my average balance in bolívares so far this month, and how much was lost to devaluation against the BCV rate." Reverón can query the information, run the calculations, explain the methodology, and show how it arrived at the result.

Third, through **bulk actions**. Reverón doesn't just analyze — it also helps you execute. For example: it can find movements that haven't been categorized or that have no counterparty assigned, identify patterns, propose rules, and help the team apply those rules to automate similar movements going forward. *And because we're talking about executing, control is always yours: Reverón proposes, and sensitive actions — the ones that move or change something important — go through your team's approval before they run. It never acts blindly.*

*And there's something that makes Reverón fundamentally different: it **learns**. Every time your team teaches it a rule, a counterparty, a preference — what you call each bank, which rate you use, how you classify a movement — it remembers, and it corrects itself when something changes. That knowledge stops living in one person's head or in a manual nobody updates, and starts living in Reverón. The more your team uses it, the more it knows your operation, and the more valuable it becomes.*

*And all of that knowledge your company builds is **yours**. Reverón is designed to be independent of whichever AI model runs underneath it — one today, the best one available tomorrow — without you losing any of what it has learned. You're not betting on a single technology; you're building an asset that belongs to your company.*

The important difference is that Reverón is not designed as a generic chatbot. It's designed to operate on the real information that already exists in each client's Tesote workspace. It runs on structured queries against the database, verifiable calculations, and traceability. Reverón must always be able to show what it did, what information it used, and how it reached each answer. *And your information stays with you: it's never used to train models, never mixed with another company's, and never leaves your space.*

This is only the beginning.

On our immediate roadmap we have integrations with ERPs, Gmail, Outlook, and other key tools, so that Reverón doesn't only understand your banking information but can also connect it with the rest of the company's financial operation.

And as always, this comes with the Tesote team alongside you. Because we know that artificial intelligence isn't adopted through technology alone. It's adopted through process, guidance, judgment, and change management.

For us, the ROI of any investment always has to be tangible. With Reverón, we believe that return can be enormous: fewer hours of manual work, fewer operational errors, faster analysis, better decisions, and a completely new capacity for finance teams to operate with artificial intelligence.

Reverón is Tesote's first major step toward a new era: one in which financial information isn't only centralized, but understood, analyzed, and executed automatically.
