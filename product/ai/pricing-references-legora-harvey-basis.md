# Pricing references for the Tesote AI launch — Legora, Harvey, Basis

Reference input for [[ai-launch-master-plan]]. Deep-research output (fan-out web search + adversarial verification), current as of June 2026.

**Bottom line:** All three run **enterprise contact-sales, no published list price**. None expose self-serve pricing; all gate behind demos/waitlists. The category norm is **per-seat annual licensing with seat minimums**, value sold on **hours-saved / efficiency-gain**, *not* consumption metering. Every dollar figure below is a third-party estimate — no vendor confirms pricing, and the adversarial verification panel killed the more precise numbers (see ["Don't cite"](#dont-cite-as-fact)).

---

## Harvey (legal AI) — the clearest model · confidence: high

- **Model:** Pure **per-seat, per-lawyer, annual** licensing. No usage/consumption metering — usage is **unlimited within a seat**. No hybrid, no credits. Notably, Harvey shipped 500 pre-built agents + an Agent Builder in May 2026 and *did not* change the per-seat model.
- **Contract:** Annual commitment + **minimum seat threshold** for enterprise deals. Pilots only — no free tier, no trial, no solo licensing.
- **Price (estimates only):** Forum/analyst chatter converges on **~$1,000–$1,200 per lawyer per month** for mid-market. Band runs wider at the extremes (lower at very high volume, higher for Am Law 100, more with the Lexis add-on).
- **Value framing:** ROI calculator — **"37 hours saved/month for power users,"** converted into billable work and profit using "conservative assumptions," citing customer data + independent RSGI research. Explicit disclaimer: *estimates only, no guarantee.* Self-reported marketing, not audited.

## Legora (legal AI) — mirrors Harvey structurally · confidence: medium

- **Model:** **Per-seat annual** with a **mandatory multi-seat minimum**. No free trial, no individual licensing. Pricing **unpublished, quote-only** — demo request is the entry point.
- **Price:** Every specific dollar figure failed verification — use only the *structure*, not the numbers.

## Basis (AI accounting) — discloses nothing · confidence: high

- **Model:** **Zero public pricing** — no per-seat, usage, or platform figure anywhere. Access is **waitlist + "Get in touch."** Enterprise/direct-sales motion aimed at top accounting firms (~30% of Top 25). Raised $100M at $1.15B (Accel/GV/Khosla, Feb 2026) and still published no price.
- **Value framing:** Substitutes an **efficiency metric for a price** — *"driving 20%–50% efficiencies across practices"* is the headline value claim (attributed to Khosla, inside Basis's own release).
- **Name-collision trap:** `getbasis.ai` (AI accounting agents) ≠ `basis.so` (FP&A "Strategic Finance Platform," which says it does *not* charge per seat). Don't conflate — only the former is the accounting-AI company.

---

## What this means for Tesote

1. **Opacity is the category default.** "Contact sales / request a demo," no rate card, is normal and credible at the high end. We're not obligated to publish.
2. **Per-seat annual + seat minimum is the reference architecture** — not consumption/usage metering. The verification panel actively *refuted* the popular "per-agent $150–300/mo" and "AI captures 2–5× the seat price" industry claims, so don't anchor on agent-metered pricing just because it's fashionable.
3. **Sell the number you *can* stand behind: a value metric, not a price.** Harvey = hours saved; Basis = % efficiency. For finance-automation, our equivalent is **reconciliation hours saved / cobros accelerated / FTE-equivalent** — same play, denominated in finance ops.
4. **LATAM caveat:** none of these operate in LATAM or fintech. They're **pricing-architecture and value-framing templates only** — they say nothing about VE/LATAM willingness-to-pay or USD-vs-Bs localization. That gap is ours to fill.
