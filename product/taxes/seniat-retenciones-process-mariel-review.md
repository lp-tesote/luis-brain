## Why this matters (business problem)

The feature isn't "automate comprobantes" for product elegance — it's **unlocking stuck tax credits**.

**The problem.** Every customer of ours (PJ Especial selling to other Especiales) is being retained on every invoice they collect. When comprobantes don't arrive on time (or never arrive), our customer:

- Can't claim the retained IVA as crédito fiscal in their monthly declaration.
- Can't claim the retained ISLR as rebaja in their annual declaration.
- AR stays permanently open for the retained amount.
- Ends up sitting on **tens or hundreds of thousands of dollars** in unused tax credits.

**Size of the problem.** An Especial invoicing ~US$1M/month is being retained ~14% (12% IVA + ~2% ISLR) = ~US$140k/month in retenidos. If only 60% of comprobantes arrive on time, that's **~US$56k/month (~US$670k/year) stuck** as unused tax credit or open AR. Scales linearly with customer size.

**The opportunity.** We help our customer help THEIR customer (the agente de retención) issue and deliver the comprobantes — Tesote turns today's human friction ("remind the customer's accountant to send the comprobantes") into an automated flow.

For our customer: unlocked tax credits, closed AR, clean books. For their customer: effortless compliance. For Tesote: sticky feature that ties both sides of the transaction to the product. Every customer we onboard makes Tesote more valuable to their counterparties.

## Worked example (used throughout)

Negotiated price: **100,000 Bs** (neto, before IVA). This is the canonical example below — every scenario in the matrix uses this same neto so we can compare apples to apples.

| Line | Amount (Bs) |
|------|------------:|
| Neto (base imponible)        | 100,000.00 |
| IVA 16%                      |  16,000.00 |
| **Total factura**            | **116,000.00** |

What gets withheld depends entirely on **who the customer is** and **who the vendor is**. See matrix below.

## The flow in plain language

Walked through with the worked example. **Tesote as the vendor** being paid by a Venezuelan client that's a Contribuyente Especial (the most common case for our target market).

### Step 1 — Invoice
Tesote issues a factura fiscal: 100,000 + 16,000 IVA = **116,000 Bs**.

### Step 2 — Customer calculates retenciones at payment time
Customer (acting as **agente de retención**) calculates:
- **Ret. IVA** = 75% × 16,000 = **12,000 Bs** (effective ~12% of net).
- **Ret. ISLR** = 2% × 100,000 = **2,000 Bs** (concepto = "servicios", Tesote is PJ domiciliada).
- **Ret. municipal** if customer is in a municipio with ordenanza (Chacao, Baruta, etc.) — typically <1% on net.

Total withheld in this canonical case: **14,000 Bs**.

### Step 3 — Customer pays Tesote NET
Customer wires Tesote: 116,000 − 14,000 = **102,000 Bs**.

### Step 4 — Customer issues comprobantes back to Tesote
- **Comprobante de Ret. IVA** — generated **inside the SENIAT portal** once the customer declares the quincena. Number format `YYYYMM########`. Must be sent to Tesote within **3 working days** after the quincena closes.
- **Comprobante de Ret. ISLR** — generated by the customer's own ERP / accounting (SENIAT does not issue this). Has all legal fields (RIF payer/supplier, factura ref, concepto, base, %, retenido, fecha).

### Step 5 — Customer declares + remits to SENIAT
Customer logs into SENIAT portal on the corresponding due date:
- **IVA** — quincenal (period 1 = days 1–15, period 2 = days 16–end). Specific due date by RIF terminal digit per the **calendario de contribuyentes especiales**. Upload via TXT batch with all retentions for the quincena.
- **ISLR** — mensual, declared in the first working days of the following month.

### Step 6 — Pay the tax through a banco recaudador
SENIAT portal generates a **planilla de pago** after declaration. Customer pays via their bank's portal / banca electrónica selecting "pago a SENIAT" against the planilla. Bancos recaudadores authorized: BBVA Provincial, Banesco, BdV, BNC, etc.

### Step 7 — Tesote's side: claim the credits
Tesote does **not** upload anything to SENIAT for these specific retentions. Tesote uses the received comprobantes as:
- Crédito fiscal in Tesote's own **monthly IVA declaration**.
- Rebaja in Tesote's **annual ISLR declaration**.

If the comprobantes never arrive → Tesote can't claim the credit → AR for that invoice stays open forever for the 14,000 Bs.

## Scenario matrix

All scenarios assume the same negotiated price = **100,000 Bs neto** (so total invoice = 116,000 Bs if vendor charges IVA). Concepto = "servicios" unless noted.

| # | Customer (payer)                  | Vendor (Tesote in default)                  | IVA charged? | Ret. IVA      | Ret. ISLR             | Cash to vendor | Comprobantes received |
|---|-----------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|--------------|---------------|-----------------------|---------------:|-----------------------|
| **A** | **Contribuyente Especial (PJ)**   | PJ domiciliada — Especial (Tesote default)  | Yes (16,000) | 12,000 (75%)  | 2,000 (2%)            |   **102,000**  | IVA + ISLR            |
| **B** | PJ NO especial                    | PJ domiciliada — Especial                   | Yes (16,000) | **0**         | 2,000 (2%)            |   **114,000**  | ISLR only             |
| **C** | Persona natural (final consumer)  | PJ domiciliada — Especial                   | Yes (16,000) | 0             | 0                     |   **116,000**  | none                  |
| **D** | Persona natural designada agente  | PJ domiciliada — Especial                   | Yes (16,000) | 0 (PN ≠ esp.) | 2,000 (2%) 🟡         |   **114,000**  | ISLR only             |
| **E** | Contribuyente Especial            | PJ domiciliada **NO** especial              | Yes (16,000) | 12,000 (75%)  | 2,000 (2%)            |   **102,000**  | IVA + ISLR            |
| **F** | Contribuyente Especial            | **Persona natural domiciliada** (freelancer, honorarios) 🟡 | Depends on PN's status (formal/ordinario) | If IVA charged: 75% of IVA | `(100,000 × 3%) − sustraendo` 🟡 | varies | 1–2                   |
| **G** | Contribuyente Especial            | **NO domiciliada** (foreign vendor)         | Special handling — typically ret. 100% IVA | 16,000 (100%) 🟡 | Higher rate, presumptive base (~27%–34%) 🟡 | drastically reduced | special               |
| **H** | Contribuyente Especial            | PJ Especial — **but Tesote's RIF not registered in SENIAT** (edge) | Yes (16,000) | **16,000 (100%)** | 2,000 (2%) | **98,000** | IVA + ISLR (with 100% retenido note) |

🟡 = needs Mariel's confirmation. The PN-supplier and no-domiciliada cases (F, G) involve formulas and rates I have not validated against current Decreto 1808 / UT values.

### Notes per scenario

**A — Default case (Tesote selling to CAPCA, Mimesa, Fospuca, etc.).** The flow described above. Two comprobantes back, AR closes only when both are received.

**B — Tesote selling to a smaller / non-especial business.** Customer is a normal company that's not designated especial → does not retain IVA. ISLR retention still applies because Decreto 1808 obligates *all* legal persons to retain on retainable conceptos. Tesote receives more cash up front but still gets one comprobante (ISLR).

**C — Tesote selling B2C to an individual.** Most retail / direct-consumer cases. No retention. Tesote gets the full 116,000 Bs. *(Mariel — please confirm this is correct for ordinary individuals; designated PN agentes de retención are the exception in case D.)*

**D — Persona natural designated as agente de retención.** Edge case. Some PN comerciantes are designated. They generally retain ISLR but not IVA (since IVA retention is restricted to especiales). Need Mariel to confirm whether this happens in practice for our customer profile.

**E — Customer is especial, vendor is not especial.** IVA retention still applies because what triggers IVA retention is the *customer's* designation, not the vendor's. ISLR same as default.

**F — Vendor is a persona natural (freelancer / consultant).** ISLR uses the formula `(base × %) − sustraendo`, with a base de exención (typically 83.3334 UT). Rate for honorarios profesionales PN domiciliada is commonly 3%. IVA: only if PN is contribuyente ordinario. Most small freelancers are contribuyentes formales (no IVA) — in that case there's no IVA to retain. *Mariel — please confirm current sustraendo computation and whether 3% / formula is still right under current Decreto 1808.*

**G — Vendor is no domiciliada (foreign supplier).** Common for SaaS imports, foreign consulting. IVA: typically 100% retained. ISLR: presumptive base × higher rate. Treatment of double-tax treaties may modify. *Mariel — flag whether this comes up for our customers and how it's handled in practice.*

**H — Edge: 100% IVA retention.** Triggered when (per existing KB):
- Tesote's RIF is not registered / not found in SENIAT portal.
- Invoice fails formal requirements (wrong numeración, missing RIF, non-authorized imprenta, no electronic invoice when required).
- Tesote is also especial but didn't charge / discriminate IVA correctly.

In our example, Tesote receives 98,000 Bs cash (vs. 102,000 in the default). This is the "invoice hygiene" failure mode worth a flag in product UX.

## Assertions for Mariel to confirm

> Please mark each as ✅ correct / ❌ wrong / 🟡 needs nuance.

### A. Who creates the comprobante

- **A1.** The **customer (payer)** creates BOTH the IVA and ISLR comprobantes. Tesote (vendor) creates neither.
- **A2.** The IVA comprobante number is **assigned by SENIAT** when the customer uploads the quincenal TXT batch — it's not a number the customer picks.
- **A3.** The ISLR comprobante is created entirely in the customer's ERP / accounting software — SENIAT doesn't assign a number for it.

### B. Who uploads to SENIAT

- **B1.** Only the **customer** (agente de retención) uploads/declares retentions to SENIAT. Tesote (vendor) does **NOT** upload these comprobantes to SENIAT — Tesote just uses them as tax credits in its own declarations.
  - 🚨 *Luis's original framing said "both Tesote and the customer upload to SENIAT" — based on the existing KB this is incorrect. Please confirm.*
- **B2.** IVA upload cadence = quincenal (every 15 days), ISLR upload cadence = mensual.
- **B3.** Upload due dates follow the SENIAT **calendario de contribuyentes especiales** by RIF terminal digit.

### C. The bank-payment leg

- **C1.** After declaring on the SENIAT portal, the customer gets a **planilla de pago** with an amount due and a reference number.
- **C2.** The customer pays it through their bank's portal via "pago a SENIAT" / "tributos nacionales", referencing the planilla.
- **C3.** Bancos recaudadores currently authorized for SENIAT collection include: BBVA Provincial, Banesco, BdV, BNC. *(Mariel — please confirm/update list.)*
- **C4.** Payment must be made by the same due date as the declaration (or within a tight window of it — please clarify).

### D. Tesote's own obligations as vendor

- **D1.** Tesote does **not** declare individual comprobantes received. Tesote uses them in aggregate when filing its own monthly IVA / annual ISLR.
- **D2.** Without the comprobante in hand, Tesote cannot claim the credit → permanent AR open balance for the retained amount.
- **D3.** If a customer classifies Tesote as "honorarios profesionales" (5% ISLR) instead of "servicios" (2%), Tesote needs to catch and dispute on AR.

### E. Edge cases worth confirming

- **E1.** **100% IVA retention** triggers when Tesote's RIF is not registered/found in SENIAT, or when the invoice fails formal requirements. Still active in 2026?
- **E2.** **Anulaciones / corrections** — what's the actual flow if the customer filed a wrong comprobante (wrong amount, concepto)?
- **E3.** **Pago en divisas** — when a customer pays Tesote in USD, retentions are calculated in Bs at what FX rate (BCV del día? día de pago? día de factura?)?
- **E4.** **Persona natural supplier (Scenario F)** — confirm current ISLR formula, sustraendo, and base de exención. Confirm IVA treatment when PN is contribuyente formal vs. ordinario.

## Two angles of the same flow

The same process looks different depending on which side of the transaction you stand on. Tesote Pay can sit on either:

**Angle 1 — The collected-from side (agente retenido). The primary opportunity.**
Our customer is being retained by their downstream customers. Tesote makes it easy for those downstream customers to issue and deliver comprobantes back to our customer automatically. This unlocks the stuck tax credits (see "Why this matters"). In the walkthrough above: imagine "Tesote" in Steps 1, 4-receiving, and 7 is actually one of our customers like CAPCA or Mimesa, and "the customer" is one of THEIR customers.

**Angle 2 — The paying side (agente de retención).**
Our customer pays their suppliers, withholding IVA + ISLR. Tesote generates the comprobantes correctly and delivers them to the supplier. This is what the feature in [[knowledge-base/product/seniat/comprobantes-automation]] covers as it stands today.

Both angles use the same mechanics (Steps 1–7 above), only who's who changes. But **Angle 1 is where the economic urgency is** for our customers — they feel the pain of stuck tax credits every month. Angle 2 is incremental value but less critical (their cash flow isn't affected the same way).

**Implication for the spec.** We need to reconsider whether the current feature prioritizes the right side. The doc at [[knowledge-base/product/seniat/comprobantes-automation]] is written from Angle 2; Angle 1 might be the first release.
