## Finding 1 — Mid-deal repricing killed deals (the Tesote 2.0 transition cost)

In at least 3 deals the price roughly **doubled while the deal was in flight** (Kiri verbatim: *"prácticamente el doble de la primera propuesta"*; Aromacom $9.6k→$18k/yr; Maxipet's wandering anchor). The Q1 consultative repositioning + full-group scoping is a legitimate upmarket move — but applying it to deals that already had a cheaper number in hand converted near-signings into stalls. Aromacom had a **January signing path** that the re-pitch un-signed.

**Decision for Luis:** if the repricing was deliberate strategy, some of these are acceptable churn of the old segment — but Kiri was exactly the new-segment target ($60k/yr full group) and was lost to a cheaper local provider. Grandfather in-flight deals at quoted price; new positioning starts with new pipeline.

## Finding 2 — The decision happens in a room we're never in

In **6 of 8** deals the kill decision was made by a junta/directiva/socios who never attended a Tesote meeting (Apolo directors, MAPLOCA junta, Aromacom's Yoli, Flor de Aragua junta, Maxipet socia, Kiri's roadmap committee). We sell to operators; owners say no. The champion walks into that room with a PDF and our price — and without our answers to the two questions that recur there: *why not just hire someone* and *why would I give a SaaS my bank credentials*.

**Fix:** junta kit (1-pager: security architecture + SOC 2 trajectory, named references, ROI framed vs. headcount) + a rule that any deal >$2k/mo gets a Luis/Roberto touch *before* the board date, not after the stall.

## Finding 3 — The proposal email is where deals go to die

Pattern across all 8: proposal email sent → zero or near-zero logged activity → closed lost days-to-weeks later. No second proposal versions exist anywhere in the record (Avanza's was explicitly promised on the call and never logged). One-shot proposals at $18–48k/yr.

**Fix:** post-proposal SLA — +48h call, +7d objection session, and a price objection *mandatorily* triggers a v2 (phased/starter) within 48h. Track "proposal versions per deal" as a metric; today it's 1.0.

## Finding 4 — The real competitor is headcount (and one named software rival)

- **Headcount:** MAPLOCA explicit, Maxipet/Salomé implicit. In VE, $1.5–2.5k/mo equals a finance clerk. Time-saved ROI (the STAR analysis) loses that math. The winning frame is what a hire *can't* do: real-time consolidated availability, data integrity, scale without fraud/error surface.
- **ENIAC** (Venezuelan, took Kiri): first named software loss in this set. Needs an intel file. SAP integration may be their wedge — ours is API-docs-DIY, theirs possibly done-for-you.
- **Security/credentials** is a recurring structural objection (Apolo fatal, Aromacom 2x docs). Advanced-permissions + consulta-only bank users exist as answers — they're arriving reactively, after hesitation, instead of in the proposal packet.

## Immediate actions

1. **Maxipet re-engagement this month** — they said June; hook = the bank-commissions analysis from 02-10.
2. **ENIAC intel doc** + Kiri check-in ~Q3 (implementation-pain window).
3. **Starter tier** (core accounts, ~$1–1.5k/mo, limited credentials) as the standard price-objection counter — three clients effectively asked for it (Apolo, Flor de Aragua, Avanza).
4. CRM hygiene: log WhatsApp outcomes as notes (the decisive 2–6 week endgame of every deal here is invisible); require detailed_loss_reason to name *who* decided and *against what alternative*.
