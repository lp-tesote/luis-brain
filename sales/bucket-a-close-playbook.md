# Bucket A Close Playbook — Tesote AI

> The zero-ceremony in-room close for the AI launch ([[../product/ai/launch-master-plan]] §7, owned by Nicolás, Esteban backs). Bucket A = instant-yes customers who hit the trial limit fast. This is the choreography to walk in, run the demo, and **leave with a signature.** Pricing model: [[../product/ai/pricing]]. Demo prompts: [[../product/dashboard/reports-catalog]].

---

## The narrative arc — "you already built the expensive thing; now flip the switch"

The pitch is **not** "buy AI." It's that the data centralization was Act 1, the AI is Act 2, and Act 2 is only possible *because* of Act 1. That framing justifies the price, *is* the moat, and makes the AI feel inevitable.

**Act 1 — remind them they own the asset:**
> *"Llevamos un año conectándote los bancos, trayéndote los movimientos en tiempo real, categorizándote todo automáticamente. Ya no persigues la data — la tienes centralizada, limpia, al día."*

**The hinge — name the gap they don't see:**
> *"Pero fíjate: la data está centralizada… el trabajo no. Tu equipo todavía exporta a Excel, arma el reporte de la mañana a mano, contesta '¿cuánto le pagamos a X?' buscando a pie. La plomería la resolvimos. El pensar encima de esa data sigue siendo manual."*

**Act 2 — the AI is leverage on the asset, not a new asset:**
> *"Eso cambia ahora. Como la data ya está centralizada y limpia, un agente puede hacer ese trabajo de pensar. Por eso son solo $2k más y no el precio de un producto nuevo: ya pagaste la parte cara. Nadie te puede vender esta IA barata porque nadie más tiene tu base de datos — la construimos contigo."*

The kill shot: **the AI is cheap *for them* and impossible *for a competitor*, for the same reason** — the data foundation already exists and only Tesote has it.

## The ROI — the manual layer on top of Tesote's data

The work the finance team does *by hand today*, using Tesote's banking data as input, that the agent eats. Split into **the floor** (labor — defensible, ~break-even) and **the upside** (leakage/FX/speed — the real money). In VE, **lead with the upside** — labor is cheap, FX mistakes are not.

### The floor — labor (defensible, ~break-even)

| Manual job today | Costs now | AI version | Quantify |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Posición diaria** rebuilt in Excel, Bs→USD, distributed | 1–2 hrs *every* morning, stale on arrival | Pushed to Slack at 7am, current, FX applied | ~300–400 analyst hrs/yr |
| **"Pásame X"** ad-hoc questions | 5–10 hrs/wk; CFO waits hours | CFO asks directly, instant | ~250–500 hrs/yr + CFO stops waiting |
| **Reportes recurrentes** (flujo, burn, por banco) | ~1–2 days/month | On demand | ~15–25 days/yr |
| **Cola de categorización + reglas** | hrs/wk + dirty data | AI clears tail, proposes rules | hrs/wk + cleaner everything |

### The upside — "found money" (lead with this in VE)

| What they can't do today | Impact | Quantify |
|---|---|---|
| **Errores / duplicados / débitos raros** — eyeballed or missed | Duplicated payment, wrong amount, unauthorized debit | One catch covers the year |
| **Exposición y timing de FX (Bs/USD)** | Converting a large balance days earlier in a devaluing-Bs world | One good timing call dwarfs the subscription |
| **Caja ociosa / fragmentada** | Money not working or exposed | Idle cash surfaced and put to work |

### The ROI one-liner for the close
> *"En lo operativo, esto se paga solo — te devuelve tiempo de tu equipo. Todo lo que agarra de más — un error, un timing de cambio, caja ociosa — es upside, y con que pase **una** vez al año, ya valió. La pregunta no es si cuesta $2k. Es cuánto te está costando hoy *no* tenerlo."*

Structure: **labor = break-even (can't argue it) → leakage/FX = the multiple (gets greedy) → reframe cost as the cost of *not* having it.**

## The on-the-spot close — beat by beat

### Beat 0 — the room is rigged before you walk in
- AI **already on** in their real workspace (their accounts, their data — not a sandbox).
- Demo prompts **pre-run the night before** on their data — you know which detonates and that the output isn't garbage. *Never let the magic misfire live.*
- **Pricing tier pre-computed** — you know the number before you sit down (big accounts land on Scale ~$5–6k, not the $1,500 floor).
- **5-bullet amendment open in a second tab**, signer's name already in it.

### Beat 1 — the magic is THEIR nightmare, not a feature tour
Run the prompt that hits the CFO's actual pain (see Tier-1 demo prompts in [[../product/dashboard/reports-catalog]]). E.g. the 9am multi-bank USD position, then the "find what doesn't match" delegation moment.

### Beat 2 — shut up
Let it land. Wait for the *"…coño."* Don't narrate over the wow.

### Beat 3 — translate magic into their money, in their language
> *"Eso que viste, tu equipo lo hace en dos días cada cierre. Son tres personas, doce cierres al año."*
Anchors value to **headcount**, not a chatbot.

### Beat 4 — the number, in the same breath, while it's hot
> *"Para [cuenta], con tu volumen y tu equipo, son [X] al mes. Menos de lo que te cuesta una de esas personas — y no se enferma, no renuncia, y trabaja el cierre mientras duermes."*
The number lands *below* the FTE you just made them picture.

### Beat 5 — collapse the friction before they reach for it
> *"No es un contrato nuevo ni nada que tu abogado revise dos semanas. Es una página, cinco puntos, un anexo a lo que ya tienes. Léelo ahorita."*
Reads like Stripe checkout. The absence of a legal loop *is* the close.

### Beat 6 — assumptive close + VIP urgency
> *"Si firmamos hoy, el lunes está todo tu equipo adentro. Y esto todavía no es público — estás entre los primeros. Lo que viene detrás" — Odoo, pagos, cobros — "lo ves antes que nadie."*

### Beat 7 — the two in-room objections
- **"Está caro."** → Don't defend; re-run the FTE frame, add scope (whole finance team, not one task), optionally show one more prompt hitting a different pain.
- **"Tengo que consultarlo."** → Make the consultee part of the magic: *"¿Qué le preguntarías? Pregúntaselo a la IA ahorita."* Or add their seat to the trial on the spot → second champion.

### Beat 8 — get the signature, or the next-best thing
- CFO is the signer → click-to-sign on the laptop, right there.
- CFO isn't → hard verbal yes + send in 5 min while it's hot. Same-day.

### Beat 9 — plant the next chapter
> *"Esto es solo la data bancaria. Cuando conectemos Odoo, te concilia contra la contabilidad completa. Ese es el próximo capítulo — y ya sabes dónde te sienta en el plan."*
Pre-sells the Odoo tier step-up before it ships.

---

## What breaks it in the room
1. **Magic misfires on messy data** → Beat 0 (pre-run) is non-negotiable.
2. **CFO isn't the economic buyer** → goal shifts from signature to making the CFO your internal champion (Beat 7).
3. **High volume → Scale number scares them** → flip it: high volume *is* the ROI; the number always sits below the team it replaces.

> Through-line: **the demo isn't the pitch, it's the proof — the close is just removing every reason to wait.** Price pre-computed, contract pre-open, AI already live, next chapter pre-sold.
