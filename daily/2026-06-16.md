### Pricing — [[../product/ai/pricing]] updated (was stale, dated 2026-05-19)

- **The fork named & resolved.** Three docs pulled opposite ways: cost-plus token model (old pricing.md) vs Harvey/Legora per-seat value model (references) vs the data×seats master-plan equation. Resolution: **price the workspace on banking-data volume; make seats a cap/circuit-breaker, not a headline.** Customer feels a Harvey-clean per-workspace number; data-tier sets the band internally; tokens stay invisible.
- **Why data-primary (not seat) is right — doubly.** (1) *Cost:* tx-volume drives tokens-per-task (context size), so it's a real cost proxy. (2) *Positioning:* competitors are cheap-per-seat (Claude Max $100–200/seat, Gemini now *bundled* into Workspace $7–22/seat, AI ~free). Per-seat pricing → CFO builds a spreadsheet and we lose. Workspace/volume pricing → comparison shifts to a finance-analyst FTE (~$1.5–4k/mo VE) or Harvey-class vertical AI. **Never quote $/seat out loud.**
- **The cost function & 80% guarantee.** COGS = `tasks/mo × tokens/task × blended $/token`. Two guards make worst-case boundable: **tx-tier reassignment** bounds tokens/task, **usage cap** bounds frequency. No path to <80% that doesn't trip a guard. Precondition: **routing must be live** (Haiku routine / Sonnet hard) — all-Sonnet kills the margin. Worked Starter example: $1,500 → $300 COGS → ~5,000 tasks/mo circuit breaker.
- **Launch = ONE SKU** (Workspace/Tesote-MCP), month-to-month (frictionless click-to-sign), convert to annual at renewal. Old 3-SKU table flagged superseded.
- **Connector layering future-proofs the MCP roadmap:** base meter = banking volume; Odoo (wave 2) = premium module priced for value; Gmail-class = included, just push volume. Volume pricing is connector-agnostic — adding MCPs tiers customers up *without* a new SKU.
- *Open input:* entry number must be a sane ratio to existing core MRR (~30–50% uplift = yes). Need typical core ACV to finalize.
