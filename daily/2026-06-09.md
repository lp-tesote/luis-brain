# 2026-06-09

## Morning — Pricing references for the Tesote AI launch (Legora / Harvey / Basis)

Ran deep research on how the comparable vertical-AI vendors price, as input for [[ai-launch-master-plan]]. Output saved to [[pricing-references-legora-harvey-basis]] (`product/ai/`).

**Key takeaways:**

- All three (Legora, Harvey, Basis) run **enterprise contact-sales, no published list price** — demos/waitlists only. Opacity is the category default; we're not obligated to publish a rate card.
- Reference architecture = **per-seat annual licensing + seat minimum**, *not* consumption/usage metering. Harvey is the cleanest model: per-lawyer annual, unlimited usage within a seat, no credits — and it kept that model even after shipping 500 agents + Agent Builder in May 2026.
- Value is sold on a **metric, not a price**: Harvey = "37 hrs saved/month for power users"; Basis = "20–50% efficiencies across practices." Our equivalent for finance-automation = reconciliation hours saved / cobros accelerated / FTE-equivalent.
- **Numbers are soft.** No vendor confirms pricing — every dollar figure is a third-party estimate. The adversarial panel killed the precise ones (Harvey tiered $/seat, Legora $3K/user + 10-seat floor, the "per-agent $150–300/mo" and "2–5× seat capture" industry claims). Only the *structure* survived verification, not the amounts.

**Caveat carried forward:** none of these are LATAM or fintech — pure pricing-architecture and value-framing templates. They say nothing about VE/LATAM willingness-to-pay or USD-vs-Bs localization. That gap is ours.

**Open / next:**

- Draft a **Tesote-specific pricing-model recommendation** building on this (per-seat annual vs. hybrid; how to denominate the value metric; USD-vs-Bs localization).
- Resolve whether any of these use hybrid (base + usage overage) for their *newer agentic* products — left unresolved after the industry claims were refuted.

## Late session — FinOps non-negotiables, currency-mismatch model, financing recommendation

Built a full financial operating plan from scratch this session — the goal was to stop running finances by feel and define the hard rules + a financing structure we can just *track* instead of stressing about. Five linked docs in `finance/`, capped by a standalone capstone.

**The non-negotiables (my decided numbers):**
- **Untouchable reserve = $400k**, USD, walled-off. (Strict $40k×12 = $480k; went to $400k because a committed line restores the gap — but that's only sound IF the line is actually committed, else the real floor is $480k.)
- **Burn cap = $40k/mo net**, trailing-3-month. Real net burn today ~$30k/mo.
- **Operating buffer = ~$150–250k** above the reserve.

**The insight that reframed everything — our binding constraint is USD liquidity, not total cash.** ~$40k MRR is USD, the rest (~$55k MRR + $7k impl fees) is Bs, while ~$120k/mo of costs are hard USD. Bs takes ~45–60 days to become spendable dollars. That mismatch — not the accounting net — is what actually drives the plan.
- **USD gap pinned at $80k/mo** (flat $40k USD rev vs flat $120k USD exp). All-Bs growth never closes it from the top. → **The $80k gap is THE structural number to attack** (grow USD revenue, shift cost base to Bs, shorten cycle). Each lever shrinks the float, the spread, and the FX risk at once.
- The 45–60d cycle creates a **permanent revolving float ~$120–160k** that survives breakeven (it's working capital, not loss).

**6-mo projection (5% MoM total MRR, all growth landing in Bs, USD MRR flat $40k, USD exp $120k):**
- **Breakeven ≈ December 2026.** Cumulative burn to get there ~$104k.
- **First credit-line draw ≈ July 2026** (~$5k); peak bridge draw ~$54k in November, self-repays after.
- Peak total facility (bridge + float) ~$170–212k → a **$250k line holds with the reserve untouched.**

**Recommendation = do BOTH** (this is the un-stress answer):
1. **~$250k credit line** on the US C-corp — workhorse, covers float + bridge, non-dilutive. *We don't have one yet — securing it is priority #1.*
2. **~$250–400k SAFE now** — NOT to fund routine burn (the line does that), but to rebuild a buffer above the reserve + insure the downside. We sit at ~$450k ≈ the reserve floor with ~zero buffer today — *that's* the source of the stress, and the equity is what removes it.
- Line alone = run at the floor, fragile. Equity alone = expensive dilution for a revolving need. Together = reserve never touched, line never maxed under duress, a bad month absorbed not a crisis.
