# 2026-05-12 — Tesote command-center architecture locked

## The day in three bullets

1. **First sync with Mariel on AR** surfaced the manual chaos behind Tesote's own books (8-step VE invoicing flow, Stripe used as a hand-maintained mirror, FX juggling, weekly chase). Aligned on standardizing VE billing to BCV + differential. Paused the Catherine hire.

**Decisions aligned:**
- **Priority order:** AR → bank recon → AP (AP in VE is small/manageable)
- **Pause Catherine hire** — same playbook as Stefi: get involved, restructure, then decide what role we actually need
- **Standardize VE invoicing on BCV** with rate differential pre-baked into the billed USD figure. Auto-calc the BCV adjustment between emission and payment date. Base imponible in USD stays constant; BCV-converted total adjusts at payment. Mariel: *"ya tienes doble diferencia cambiario"* — BCV + differential, both indexed.

### Sync 2 (~6 min) — Stripe deep-dive

Mariel walked the Stripe workflow:
- Emit BS invoice in Odoo VE (real billing)
- Open Stripe → duplicate a similar prior invoice → modify period/date → finalize as "informational"
- When payment lands, reconcile in BOTH Odoo (real) and Stripe (mirror) — mark paid + add payment method ("Binance Bench") + date

**The realization that changed everything:** Stripe was never doing master record-keeping for VE. It was manual aggregation labor maintaining a parallel ledger so we had something that *looked* unified across entities. The "duplicate prior invoice → modify" pattern is itself a manual workaround for recurring subscriptions.

Mariel pointed directly at the Tesote product: *"tienes un módulo de facturación, también tienes las transacciones"* — the surfaces Tesote needs to replace Stripe's aggregator role already exist on the product side. **This is the moment the architecture clicked.**

## The architectural decision

**Tesote = command center. Odoo = execution layer.**

- Tesote is the **first landing place** for everything: invoices (inbound + outbound), contracts, retention vouchers, receipts — anything with finance metadata
- Tesote is the **only UI** the end user sees. Odoo becomes invisible to Mariel (and to our customers).
- Tesote **controls and executes** Odoo: legal invoices, journal entries, tax compliance, fiscalización
- Applies to: Odoo VE (now), Odoo DR (now), eventually Odoo US (post-QB migration)
- Stripe drops out of cross-entity SoR role entirely; stays only as US card processor

|---|---|---|

| 2 | Inbound channel design | Email-as-inbox + upload portal for v1. Vendor portal integrations as v2. |

| 6 | Multi-entity counterparty | Day-one requirement. One counterparty, N entity-billing relationships. |

## Why this matters strategically

**Three things landed today that change Tesote's 2026 trajectory:**

1. **Dogfooding as North Star.** Tesote's own finance ops (Mariel) IS the pilot customer. Our pain = customer's pain. The command center we build for ourselves IS the product. Validation and shipping converge.

2. **The Stripe finding collapses the build cost.** A/B sequencing question I posed earlier in the day (Tesote-as-SoR vs Tesote-as-aggregator) dissolves because Stripe was never the real SoR for VE. We're replacing manual labor, not a working system. Path A is cheaper than B.

3. **AI changes the cost structure.** Three years of feature work — extraction, categorization, anomaly detection, chase prioritization — compresses into utilities we layer across workflows. No dedicated team to build a separate "AI product."

The QB → Odoo US migration becomes a strategic dependency for unifying the US entity into the command center (not just a bookkeeping migration). Worth flagging on the 2026 plan.

## Late evening — BNC débito inmediato unpacking + DI003 ticket

BNC replied via WhatsApp to the 2026-05-10 follow-up (4 questions on the débito inmediato BCV rail). Unpacked their responses, posted to PAY-7, opened sibling ticket PAY-10, broadcast to the team in #payments.

**Three takeaways:**

1. **OTP delivery hypothesis confirmed with a twist.** BNC says the payer's bank is the only OTP channel, and the OTP routes via the affiliated phone (typically the pago móvil PJ phone). The 9 banks where OTP didn't land may not be "banks that don't support PJ-OTP" — they may be VDT accounts without pago móvil PJ affiliation. Cheap diagnostic before declaring anything non-viable.
2. **Interbank traceability is structurally limited.** BNC can't tell whether an SMS/push actually reached the payer's device — they only see that a notification process was initiated. Means BNC is not a viable support escalation for "I never got the OTP." Need a per-payer-bank contact map separately.
3. **Domiciliación (DI003) is a different primitive.** Not a flag on the same request — it's a contract-based mandate with a 7-day payer-approval window, a deliberate first-cobro-rejection (MD01) bootstrap pattern, and a separate state-code family (MD01/MD09/MD22). Opened PAY-10 to cover validation + product design (data model + UX).

**Detail worth pinning down on DI002 (OTP):**
- 2-minute token TTL → UX constraint for any DI002 flow
- ACCP ≠ settled (means funds blocked pending liquidation, not terminal-success)
