**The path (cheap iterations, isolated canvases first):** posición-box v1 (extracted) → v2 (USD/VES promoted to boxes, desglose opened) → a 10-up composition gallery → **graph-forward gallery (10)** where Luis picked **v8** (area chart + decomposition band) → **v8 final gallery (10)** where he picked **v1** (gradient area + beginning-balance dot, clean band) → folded into the full page.

**Design decisions locked this session — most are page-agnostic RULES (feed the future global design-system file, see action item):**
- **Multi-currency display rule:** USD shown in dollars; **VES shown in Bs as the primary number with the dollarized `≈ $X` directly beneath.** Bs-first wherever a VES balance appears. (USD stays the consolidated protagonist.)
- **Position chart:** large gradient area over 30 d; **beginning balance marked on the curve** (start dot + value, *no* "hace 30 d" caption); current point dotted. Reconciles to the band.
- **Δ / decomposition band:** order **Entró · Salió · Variación neta · Devaluación**; must **reconcile to the chart** (begin $490.550 + neta $33.190 − devaluación $1.900 = today $521.840 = the `+$31.290 · 30 d` chip). Devaluación always lives on the VES side.
- **Every time-bound box states its window explicitly** (chart/band = 30 d, Entró/salió KPI = *week* w/ dates, cash-flow = *month*). Fixed a bug where the weekly KPI showed the band's exact 30-d numbers → de-conflicted to a real weekly subset.
- **Cash-flow report = direct method** (créditos − débitos grouped by category, net-flow headline) — replaces flat category bars. This is the "Categorías" box's real form.
- **Saldos por cuenta = a list** (not a grid); accounts pulled out of the hero into their own bottom list. Hero Desglose trimmed to **banco + entidad** only.
- **Chrome discipline:** killed the "Desglose" label (column titles self-label); removed redundant dividers — keep only the chart→band line; tightened all hero gaps so the **box + AI read end-to-end on load**.
- **Workspace identity in the page head:** **logo slot + workspace name** ("Tesote Ventas"), replacing the personal "Hola, Mariel" greeting. Personal identity stays in the sidebar user; the AI greets by name. Logo is an uploadable mark.
- **Tesote AI panel** (lives in the hero's right column, ~1fr beside the box): name is **Tesote AI** (English, *not* "IA"; "La IA" = marketing/colloquial only), **no "Copiloto" badge**. Structure = catchy **serif voice line** (Claude-style, the locked `/ai` serif identity) + **pre-generated week-to-date report** (insight-led) + **3 prompt buttons** + input.
