# 2026-06-14

## Session — Reverón brand + landing page

**Workstream:** AI product branding + marketing landing (new brand home at `marketing/reveron/`).

### Decisions

- **Named the AI product "Reverón, por Tesote AI."** Lab model: agent = Reverón (a *someone* you delegate to), maker/lab = "Tesote AI" (signals it's AI + we have an AI lab, without renaming the company — company stays Tesote). Same pattern as Claude/Anthropic, ChatGPT/OpenAI. Accent kept. `reveron.ai` domain already owned; assuming trademark/domain cleared.
  - My pushback: the namesake (Armando Reverón = *"el loco de Macuto"*) risks an instability connotation, bad for a finance tool; and **Rever.ai** is a near-homophone AI-reconciliation product in the same category. Luis's call: it clicked after browsing hundreds, keep it. Resolution = reframe the "loco" into **obsessive clarity / light / Venezuelan genius** ("lo llamaron loco porque nadie miraba la luz tan de cerca").
- **Value prop framing:** Reverón does the finance work (conciliar, categorizar, reportar, automatizar) and hands you a *decision* — **"Tú solo apruebas."** Agentic, human-in-the-loop = the trust story for finance. CFO-altitude: sell the outcome (control, decisions, time), not the operator task.
- **Landing page LOCKED for today = [[landing-v4]]** (`marketing/reveron/landing-v4.html`). Centered, one-screen/no-scroll, **platinum/brushed-metal field + black Instrument Serif wordmark** (letterpress emboss), copy-only (no product image). **Final for today — do not reopen the layout.**

- v4: collapsed to **one screen, no scroll**; inverted to **platinum bg / black letters**; sized up "por Tesote AI". **← locked.**
- Rejected: v5 split (copy left / app right), `hero-options.html` (20 approval-card heroes), `hero-app-options.html` (20 full-app heroes), `hero-wordmark-options.html` (20 giant-wordmark + small-app). Luis called the small-app-in-wordmark layouts "hideous." Kept on disk, marked rejected.
