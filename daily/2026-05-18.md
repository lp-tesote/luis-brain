## The plumbing problem (the hard part)

Three data/system gates blocking the "Tesote runs on Tesote" story:

### 1. Venezuela — old data, months unreconciled

Most complicated part of the JTBD. The VE side of the house has unreconciled txns going back months. Have to **finish cleaning that up and get everything reconciled and tidy** before the dogfood demo holds water. You can't demo "Tesote = finance command center" on top of a messy ledger.

### 2. Dominican Republic — Odoo access

Need to **get access to our old DR Odoo instance** so we can run DR subscriptions through it too. Without this, subs are partial.

### 3. United States — Stripe + QuickBooks → new Odoo US instance

For the Delaware C-corp ([[reference_tesote_legal_entities]]: TESOTE TECHNOLOGIES INC.), we need to **stand up a new Odoo US instance** and **transition Stripe + QuickBooks into it**. This is the cleanest path — Odoo becomes the system of regulatory record for US too, mirroring what we have in VE.

End state across all three jurisdictions: Odoo posts the facts, Tesote is the system of action on top. Consistent with [[project_tesote_vs_odoo_split]].

## Why this is the right bet this week

- **No demo without dogfood.** Until our own finance team is running fully on Tesote, every sales call is hand-wavy. "We use it ourselves" is the only credible opening.

- **Subscriptions v1 is the unlock for the subs GTM.** Until we run our own subs through it, we can't sell it.
- **Everything else is secondary.** Anything that isn't (a) page redesigns, (b) Odoo invoices in Cobros/Pagos, (c) Subs v1, or (d) the three plumbing fixes — deprioritize.

## EOD update — workstream cut rationale + open seams

Capturing the *why* behind the workstream cut, not just the map. For future-Luis (and Dan if he asks why these splits).

### Why 6 workstreams (3 build / 3 plumbing)

The 3 build / 3 plumbing split followed the original framing in this note (the three targets + the three plumbing problems). Resisted the temptation to merge plumbing into a single "ops cleanup" workstream — they have different owners, different blockers, and different relationships to the demo:

- **WS-4 (VE recon)** is operational, partly Mariel, partly Dan-adjacent — blocks demo credibility on the existing VE workspace
- **WS-5 (DR access)** is access ops first, integration second — blocks WS-3 v1.1 only, NOT this week's demo
- **WS-6 (US Odoo)** is the longest pole — doesn't block anything this week, but the 5 open questions need answers now or v1.2+ slips

Splitting them keeps the demo-gate visible (WS-4) separate from the unblock-later work (WS-5, WS-6).

### Why Cobros + Pagos went into ONE PRD (WS-2)

Rejected the instinct to split into two PRDs. They share:

- The Odoo MCP plumbing (same `list_invoices` / `apply_payment` methods, just `type=ar` vs `type=ap`)
- The table primitive (Movimientos-pattern)
- The drill-in pattern
- The event-row data model (`outbound_send_events`, `payment_application_events`)

Splitting forces duplicate planning and risks UX drift. The PRD's appendix has the explicit "why one PRD" justification — `/tesote-plan` can phase implementation AR-first / AP-second within one plan dir. Domain divergence (dunning vs approval routing) kicks in at v1.1, **that's** when to split.

### Why WS-1 is an umbrella, not a single PRD

Originally drafted WS-1 as one PRD bundling Saldos retrofit + Movimientos drill-in + empty states. Refactored after rereading the Saldos-solo dry-run decision earlier today — `/tesote-plan` should run on focused surfaces, not bundled ones. Architecture-review checklists make more sense per-page.

WS-1 became the **umbrella that points at child PRDs**, not a PRD itself. The Saldos PRD will come from the paste-able prompt approach (already drafted upstream in this session); Movimientos drill-in PRD pattern-copies it after Saldos lands clean.

### Load-bearing open seams (worth pulling forward)

These showed up across multiple workstream briefs and deserve focused thinking before the build PRDs lock:

1. **Counterparty mapping gap** (WS-2 open decision #3) — Tesote ↔ Odoo partner mapping. If the dataset is messy, V0 has to decide auto-create vs block-until-mapped. Cross-references [[../product/business/counterparties/system-of-record]].

4. **Status taxonomy alignment** (WS-2 open decision #5) — adopt Odoo statuses verbatim, or normalize to Tesote vocab? Small decision, big downstream impact on UX consistency.

These are the four that I'd resolve **before** WS-2 and WS-3 hit `ready-for-tesote-plan`. Each is a 10-minute decision; collectively they shape ~3 weeks of build.

## Late evening — `_workflows/` folder decision (operating system)

Created `_workflows/` at the brain root for **operating-system-level procedures** — multi-step, cross-tool, cross-repo workflows. Trigger: just codified the brain → treasury bridge (`/tesote-plan` workflow) and realized `learnings/` was the wrong home for it. `learnings/` is "skills I'm building" (Claude Code mastery, fundraising mechanics, etc.); the `/tesote-plan` doc is "how Luis + Claude operate together" — different category.

### The three-categories framing (durable mental model)

```
function content   → strategy/ sales/ product/ finance/ ...   (what I think ABOUT)
process content    → daily/ decisions/ drafts/ people/ ...    (how I think)
operating system   → CLAUDE.md, _workflows/, ...              (how Luis + Claude work)
```

CLAUDE.md was the only operating-system surface before. Now `_workflows/` accretes the procedures that don't fit in CLAUDE.md prose. `_` prefix sorts to top of `ls`, signals "system, not content."

### Why NOT `_templates/` or `_meta/`

Templates have *domains* — a PRD template belongs in `product/`, an investor-update template in `strategy/`, a decision-memo template in `decisions/`. A single `_templates/` folder fights that gravity. Same logic engineers use putting `.editorconfig` at repo root, not in `_meta/`: keep tools near the work.

Workflows are different — they're *meta by nature*, span multiple domains, and benefit from a single discovery surface. `_workflows/` is the right shape; `_templates/` is the wrong abstraction.

**Rule:** Templates stay with their domain (`product/_prd-template.md`). Workflows live in `_workflows/`.
