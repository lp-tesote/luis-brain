## Late session — Tesote AI pricing draft

First pass at SKU pricing for Tesote AI. Doc saved at [[../product/ai/pricing]], status draft, comes back once we have ~30-60d of Mariel's actual token usage.

### SKUs + starter prices (working)

- Workspace AI — $1.5k/mo
- Odoo AI — $1.5k/mo
- Bundle — $2.5k/mo (17% discount vs standalone, to drive bundling)

These are **starter tier** (≤10k tx/mo). Decouple feature SKU from price tier: tx-volume multiplier handles the 1000x customer variance (2k → 2M tx/mo). Rough multipliers: 1.0x / 1.5x / 2-3x / custom.

### Margin target locked at 80%

Anchor for the whole model. $1.5k SKU × 20% COGS = **$300/mo token budget per workspace**. Drives everything downstream — caps, routing strategy, what overage actually has to cost.

### Why the 30% overage premium got rejected

Original idea: customers exceeding their cap pay overage tokens at +30% over our OpenRouter cost. Ran the math:

- Inside cap = 80% margin (customer pays $5 per $1 of token cost)
- +30% overage = 23% margin (customer pays $1.30 per $1)
- Overage tokens are **4x cheaper per-token than included tokens**
- → Heavy users rationally stay on starter plan and overage forever instead of upgrading

This silently turns the premium into a heavy-user discount. Bad.

### Leaning soft overage + sales motion (first 6-12 months)

Instead of mechanical overage billing, hitting the cap = a conversation: "you're a heavier user, here's the next tier." Better fit for fintech B2B w/ high-touch sales, avoids surprise-bill churn while we still don't know real cost/customer. Revisit once we have data to defend a specific overage rate (or switch to punitive 2-2.5x markup, the Twilio/AWS pattern).

### Three day-one operational prereqs

1. **Per-tenant token accounting** — hard prereq, no caps/billing without it
2. **Customer-facing usage dashboard** — % of cap used, 80%/100% alerts; surprise overage kills NPS
3. **Aggressive model routing** — Haiku for routine, Sonnet/Opus for hard reasoning. Single biggest lever on whether 80% margin is achievable.

### Caps communicated as "AI tasks", not tokens

Tokens are invisible to customers and create anxiety. Frame it as task count, or peg to tx-volume tier directly. Keep the token math internal.
