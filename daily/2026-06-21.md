### Pricing — honest take

Stress-tested [[../product/ai/pricing]] against the deal data and tesote.ai repo architecture.

**Top concerns in order:**

1. **Routing must be live before the first Scale quote.** The 80% margin is premised on Haiku-for-routine / Sonnet-for-hard. All-Sonnet → blended rate 3–4× higher → Starter margin drops to ~70%, Scale margin could go negative. Confirm with Dan: is treasury calling through the router or direct upstream?

2. **The labor anchor won't hold in VE.** *"Cheaper than one of those people"* assumes a $1,500+/mo FTE. In VE a loaded finance analyst is $300–500/mo. The close in VE should lead with FX/leakage upside (*"un timing de cambio mal hecho te cuesta más que un año de esto"*), not headcount math. Told Esteban and Nicolás explicitly.

3. **Launching without Mariel's usage data.** The $1,500 = $300 COGS premise is a well-reasoned bet, not a calculation. The first 2–3 weeks of live customers *are* the pricing research. Per-tenant token accounting must be live day one.

4. **Tier thresholds need to be validated per account before quoting.** Don't put a Scale number on a customer without checking their tx volume first.

**What I'd actually do at launch:** put every Bucket A customer on Starter ($1,500) regardless of size. Upsell the tier at month 2 when actual usage is in hand.

### Architecture → pitch spine — [[../product/ai/customer-zero-and-right-to-win]]

Read the real subsystems (not just the spec). The building blocks that make Reverón "not just an AI chat":
- **MCP gateway** — lazy 3-tool surface (`discover→describe→dispatch`); a connected server costs the model **3 schemas regardless of tool count**. Masking = same mechanism gives per-role security (hidden tool ≡ typo). The "holy shit" sell.
- **Memory / gravity layer** — *the business, not a feature*. Self-correcting (tombstones), 30-day recency decay, **graph-memory** associative recall, **citations** (auditable), no-train/exportable. It *builds the KB itself* as a byproduct of use — the answer to the Fortune-500 "implementation is the bottleneck" problem.
- Plus: live data-bound artifacts, background agent runs, sealed credentials, the payment-rail endgame.

Doc structure: market reframe → day-one/fast-follow/horizon phasing → customer-zero → primitives → gateway + memory deep dives → right-to-win → education arc. Confirmed **tesote.ai/Reverón = the product** (Claude workspace was the MVP); chassis + Tesote as first vertical via MCP. Saved to memory as [[project-tesote-ai-is-the-product]].

**Right-to-win (why us, not OpenAI):** access · integration labor · governance/trust · accumulated gravity · the rail. Meta: we're *not* betting on the best model — we rent/swap it; value moves to the layer on top. Harvey/8090 lesson.
