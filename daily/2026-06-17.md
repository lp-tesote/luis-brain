## Late session — The Confirmation Ladder framework

Same broad theme as the announcement above (bank-API transition, off scraping) — but where that's the *client-facing comms*, this is the **internal operating model**. The announcement says "we're moving off scraping"; this framework says *what we do instead and how we scope it per company.*

**Trigger:** PuroLomo question (top-10 VE food distributor, on Microsoft Business Central). They want real-time payment confirmation to release product to customers, but several banks mutate transactional info at T-1 (reference number, round cents, description — varies by bank), which breaks their matching/reconciliation. Worked the T-day/T-1 drift problem, then elevated it to a general operating model for how Tesote designs solutions going forward.

**Output:** [[../product/confirmation-ladder]] (`product/confirmation-ladder.md`) — framework-only, no client specifics. Linked from [[../product/index]]. Memory: `project_confirmation_ladder`.

**The framework:**

- **Core decision — scraping is OFF the menu.** Confirm cash only via sanctioned/durable mechanisms (real bank APIs, botón). *Why:* the moment we promise scraping-backed "real-time" for a bank that doesn't offer it, we're back in today's fragility. Tradeoff accepted: lose the "we cover everything" pitch, gain integrity + an SLA we can stand behind + a **botón attach on every real-time gap**. Scraping = cost center + liability; botón = revenue line + moat. (This is the internal articulation of the stance the client announcement is communicating.)
- **The ladder (4 rungs):**
  1. Botón de Pago / C2P — real-time + immutable, bank-agnostic. The only true real-time-*and*-clean confirmation.
  2. Real-time bank API — fast but provisional; Tesote absorbs drift behind a stable canonical ID + provisional→confirmed lifecycle.
  3. T-1 bank API — settled/clean; for reconciliation, *not* release.
  4. ~~Scraping~~ — off the menu.
  - Two traps: **real-time ≠ immutable** (rung 2 still mutates at settlement); **timely data ≠ matchable data** (overlay payer-ID/RIF availability on every rung).
- **The 5-question diagnostic:** (1) collection footprint; (2) what does the data *trigger* — release/credit/reconcile/report (sets the *real* latency need vs. what they ask for); (3) cost of wrong vs. late; (4) map footprint × ladder × payer-ID → the gap; (5) the gap is botón territory → co-design.
- **Sales motion flip:** from "can you cover bank X?" (a coverage arms race we lose to scrapers) to "what are you optimizing for, and what's the cost of latency?" (consultative — botón becomes the natural answer to the gap we just made visible). Make the constraint the reason they need the botón.

**Why this matters:** operating model behind every future solution-design conversation, not a PuroLomo note. Maps onto the command-center thesis (Tesote = system of action, ERP = system of record — here Business Central plays Odoo's role).

**Next moves:**

- Run PuroLomo through the diagnostic as worked-example #1 (its footprint + bank-nomenclature/RIF scoring already map onto steps 1 and 4).
- Strong **KB-promotion candidate** once the framework settles — durable principle the team should design against.
- Eng note: the canonical-record + provisional→confirmed lifecycle + cross-drift re-matching is real schema/services work — a `/tesote-plan` candidate (`database-design`, `product-management`) when a concrete client solution needs it.
