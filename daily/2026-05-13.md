Open question: a Spanish one-liner for Tesote's Instagram page describing what we are / what we do.

### Angles surfaced

### Luis's draft

> *"Plataforma líder para el control financiero de tu empresa: data en tiempo real, inteligencia artificial, y agentes que llevan las finanzas al día."*

The line that makes it land: **"agentes que llevan las finanzas al día"** — agents as *doers*, not assistants. That's the actual bet (matches the AI-is-utility-not-surface principle from yesterday's PRD).

### Feedback flagged on the draft

- Accents missing: *líder*, *día*
- ***data* vs *datos***: "data" reads tech-bro startup; "datos" reads native VE
- ***líder*** is the most fragile word — defensible if owned, but most challengeable on IG
- ***control* vs *operación***: control = oversight/passive; operación = doing. Agents *operate*, so "operación" is more honest. Unless "control" is the emotional CEO hook (valid play).
- ***IA* vs *inteligencia artificial***: spelled-out is heavier; IG readers skim. "IA" buys space.

### Recommendation (not yet locked)

> *"El centro de operaciones financiero de tu empresa: datos en tiempo real, IA y agentes que las llevan al día."*

**Why:** combines Luis's strong three-part proof with the locked command-center thesis as the headline noun. Drops "líder" (the fragile word). Tightens "data → datos" and "inteligencia artificial → IA". Keeps the agents-as-doers line intact.

### VE tax primer — the part I hadn't internalized

For every Bs of gasto without fiscal soporte (factura) → can't deduct from ISLR → pay **34% income tax** on it. So when I gave Dan $2K corporate card for Caracas hotel and didn't bring back a factura → that's **~$680 in tax** on what looks like a clean expense in the bank tx.

Math she walked me through:

```
Ingresos                1,000,000
- Gastos deducibles       250,000  (have facturas)
- Gastos no deducibles    250,000  (no facturas)
= Utilidad contable       500,000
+ Add back no deducibles  250,000
= Utilidad fiscal         750,000
× 34%                   = 255,000 ISLR
```

vs the "clean" version (everything deducible):
```
1,000,000 - 500,000 = 500,000 × 34% = 170,000 ISLR
```

**Delta = $85K extra tax on $250K of unsupported expense = ~34% effective penalty.** That's why Mariel is fastidiosa about facturas.

## Earlier — Tesote AI pitch doc (Agents + AI frame)

Wrote the customer-facing pitch for Tesote AI as a standalone doc at [[../product/ai/pitch-agents-plus-ai]]. ### The frame decision — two layers

The whole pitch hinges on one slide:

- **Agents** = autopilot layer. Recurring, predictable, runs itself. Smart rules, mass categorization, AP inbox, recon engine, retention auto-calc, reminder cadences. The 80% that should have been automated 10 years ago.
- **AI** = superpower layer. Prompt-driven leverage. "Categorize 3 months of bank statements." "Cobra esta semana." "Concílialo todo." "¿Estoy lista para declarar IVA?" Mass-edit hundreds of invoices, mass-reconcile, mass-anything.

**Why this frame matters:** customers *buy* Agents (trust, relief from grind), but they *fall in love* with AI (100× moments). Pitching only one of the two undersells. Pitching them as "AI" undifferentiated loses to ChatGPT-fud.

### Structure — by function, not by feature

Seven function sections, each with the same three-part shape:
1. **The Agents we have today** (what runs by itself)
2. **The AI prompts on top** (what one-prompt leverage unlocks)
3. **The wedge sentence** (the one line the buyer should walk away with)

Functions covered: Receivables · Payables · Reconciliation · Categorization & Rules · Counterparties · Close & Reporting · SENIAT / Tax compliance.

**Why by-function not by-feature:** matches how the customer's org is structured. Controller hears Receivables and Payables. CFO hears Close and SENIAT. Daily operator hears Categorization and Bandeja. Every persona finds their slot in the same pitch.

### The customer test — the only success metric

> *"If you can give me this MCP today, I can start using Odoo this way — that's all I need."*

If 3 prospects in a row say this unprompted, the pitch is right. If they say *"interesting, send the roadmap"* — pitch is wrong, recalibrate. Made this explicit at the bottom of the doc so future-Luis (or future-Claude) doesn't drift the pitch into roadmap-y abstractness.

### Honest readiness table — the line we can't lie about

Color-coded 🟢/🟡/🔴 per function so the demo doesn't oversell. The "install today" claim is only honest where the table is 🟢:

- 🟢 today: **Categorization, Counterparties** (live in app + MCP)
- 🟢 in ~2 weeks: **Reconciliation** (once `bank_statement_lines` MCP gap from [[../product/ai/tesote-workspace-mcp-feedback]] closes), **Receivables** (with copy-to-clipboard fallback on WhatsApp)
