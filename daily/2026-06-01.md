# 2026-06-01 — Strategic resequence with Daniel

## Morning sync — the resequence

Two convos with Dan today (one recorded ~50min, one earlier unrecorded + a voicenote follow-up). Full synthesis lives in [[../strategy/tesote-2026-resequence|strategy/tesote-2026-resequence]]. The raw of it:

**The keystone (from the voicenote):** the pivot is a **resource-reallocation** argument, not just "new products scale better." Web scraping is a *resource sink* — it eats dev/support/ops capacity. The team is talented but trapped servicing scrapers, so every new launch is starved. Release scraper pressure in the medium term → reallocate *existing* capacity to AI + Negocios. The unlock is **freeing** capacity, not **buying** it. That's the whole case.

- It's a **valley-crossing bet**: spend scarce capacity now (migration) to free it later, while scrapers still run AND we build both. The ~$200k cushion carries us across the valley — not growth capital.
- What stays proven regardless of rail: **data aggregation has strong PMF** (scraping or API). We change the rail + wrapper, not the core value.

**New ICP:** bank-API data + Negocios (payments) + Odoo + Tesote AI. Lead the sales convo with AI, then "where does the data come in, how often" forces the rail change downstream.

**The sequence (ordered, not equal):**
1. **Connect: scrapers → bank APIs** *(foundation — frees the capacity)*. Two separate plans: new customers = API-first day one (~zero pressure); current customers = migration/batch-1 (where pressure-relief gets *measured*). Make the 100%→20% pressure-relief a real number, not a vibe.
2. **Tesote AI** — packaging is a *ladder*: (a) standalone-for-Odoo = wedge for reps/revenue/FOMO but zero moat ([[../strategy/winning-vs-horizontal-ai|horizontal trap]]); (b) add-on to core; (c) add-on to core+Odoo = the durable destination SKU. Sell (a) now, build toward (c).
3. **Tesote Negocios** (pagos/payables/receivables) — dogfood + first customers ASAP. Dan's steer (Luis agreed): product-dev + dogfooding *over* WTP discovery this week. Bar: "tesote finance is meaningfully better with the full portal."

**Arturos = the proof case.** ~300k tx/month, majority of banks already on an API relationship with Tesote. The instrument that turns the pressure-relief hypothesis into a number. Sell as Connect (mostly APIs + some scrapers) + AI on Connect data + Odoo. If pressure-relief shows up here, the thesis is proven and the sequence locks.

**Decisions made:**
- **Kill Casagri** — offer AI+Odoo at same price, disconnect banks. (Caveat: is "they poke holes" a product-readiness signal that recurs, or a bad-fit customer? Don't let killing them hide it.)
- **Sell AI+Odoo to Oriand** — proceed, get reps.
- **Fundraising: not raising to invest.** Tap Gilgamesh ~$150–300k as a no-friction liquidity cushion → makes 1 dev + 1 PM (Caracas) hires lighter. Bet-on-ourselves, not increase-burn. Larger raise only if it provably accelerates the timeline (~2mo vs 6–8mo to sellable AI/payments).
- **Limit Sebastian's strategic input** (Dan) — "hire 3 devs now" isn't the input; problem isn't purely resourcing.

## Mid-day — Veconinter intro call + connection-options framing

Pitched the new connection story live for the first time (Veconinter intro call — *not captured by Fireflies*, reconstructed from memory + the internal debrief w/ Esteban & Roberto). Folded into [[../strategy/tesote-2026-resequence|resequence]] as an add-on section. Gist:

- **Two-bucket framing.** Bucket 1: connection options — **API (reliable, higher integrity, limited syncs/day) vs Direct (more syncs/day, but unstable + unpredictable repair work)**. Bucket 2: if aggregation is the value, satisfy more banks *without scraping* — MT940 for non-VE banks + **seamless statement upload where Tesote AI does the rest**.
- **Key reframe: aggregation > real-time.** For most customers once-a-day works. So the once-daily API tier (BBVA/Provincial, Mercantil) isn't a hole — it's fine for most. Real-time demotes to a niche.
- **Direct connections → per-bank sunset candidate.** Not sure we even want to keep selling Direct, esp. problematic banks. Decision (mine): stop-selling-to-new vs migrate-existing sequencing. Cheap check first: what % of base ever needed intraday?
- **API tier (load-bearing):** unlimited → BNC, Banesco (Exterior); once-daily → BBVA/Provincial, Mercantil — and those two are the problem banks.
- **Veconinter = first ICP filter.** Time-sensitive customs/aduana payments, need hourly sync; top banks BBVA/Mercantil/BNC (→ receivables on BNC = unlimited). ~40% ops overseas in long-tail banks → trigger for the MT940/upload idea.
- **Payment portal = unproven but real hypothesis for their real-time-confirmation problem.** Not pitched today, not proven — but textbook use case: clients pay *through* Tesote → confirmation solved by construction. Validate, don't promise.
- Esteban's sales rule: frame the real-time-bank move as a *requirement*, not a *recommendation* (recs get ignored). Bonus: pushing customers onto BNC/Banesco makes them future payments customers.
